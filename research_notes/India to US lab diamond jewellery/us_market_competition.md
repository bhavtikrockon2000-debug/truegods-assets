# US Market & Competitive Landscape: Gold Fine Jewellery with Lab-Grown Diamonds (LGD) & Moissanite (2025-2026)

> **How these notes were gathered (read first):** Notes compiled 2026-09-25. The network proxy blocked full-page fetches from JCK, National Jeweler, Tenoris, SEC and Signet, so every finding below comes from **search-result snippets and summaries**, not full articles. Numbers are reported as the snippets gave them. Treat single-source figures as needing a check against the original before anyone relies on them publicly. Where a source is a retailer blog or an aggregator rather than an analyst, that is flagged.

## Q1. US fine jewellery market size; LGD share of US jewellery and engagement-ring sales; moissanite market

### Takeaway
The US jewellery market is roughly $63-106B, depending on the definition used. It grew about 5.6% in 2025 and about 9% in H1 2026 (Tenoris). Lab-grown diamonds now make up most engagement rings (61% of 2025 buyers, per The Knot) and about 42% of diamond jewellery overall. Moissanite is a small niche, and the category's pioneer, Charles & Colvard, went bankrupt in 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Market size**
- US jewellery market size estimates vary widely by definition. IMARC: $78.9B (2025). Arizton: $78.4B (2024). Statista: about $63B (2025). FRED (US personal spending on jewellery and watches): $106.0B in 2025. ResearchAndMarkets: $105.2B (2024), projected $159.6B by 2033 — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/united-states-jewelry-market); [Arizton](https://www.arizton.com/market-reports/us-jewelry-market); [Statista](https://www.statista.com/topics/5841/jewelry-market-in-the-united-states/); [Yahoo/ResearchAndMarkets](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/united-states-jewelry-market-report-154400402.html)
- Tenoris (Edahn Golan): US jewellery sales grew 5.6% in 2025 — [Tenoris](https://www.tenoris.bi/the-us-jewelry-market-in-2025-with-5-6-sales-growth/)
- Tenoris: US jewellery sales grew 9% in H1 2026 — [Tenoris, "US Jewelry Sales Grow 9% in H1 2026"](https://www.tenoris.bi/us-jewelry-sales-continue-to-grow-in-h1-2026/)

**LGD share**
- Lab-grown stones were 42.1% of all diamond jewellery sold in the US in 2025. The snippet does not say whether this is by units or by value — [Tenoris via search summary](https://www.tenoris.bi/us-jewelry-sales-continue-to-grow-in-h1-2026/)
- Edahn Golan put LGD at 14% of total diamond sales in 2024 (all diamond sales, not just engagement). This likely measures something different from the 42.1% figure (value vs units, or loose vs jewellery) — [JCK Q&A with Edahn Golan](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/edahn-golan-lab-grown-diamonds/); [Gem Guide LGD market update, Apr 2025](https://files.gemguide.com/20250401062337/2025_AprilLGD.pdf)
- H1 2026: jewellery set with LGD posted double-digit revenue growth. Almost all of that growth came from more units sold, with average spend per purchase roughly flat. For natural diamond jewellery, units fell but average spend rose — [Tenoris H1 2026](https://www.tenoris.bi/us-jewelry-sales-continue-to-grow-in-h1-2026/)
- The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study (released Feb 18, 2026; more than 10,000 US couples married in 2025): 61% of engagement rings had a lab-grown centre stone, up 239% since 2020. 40% of couples said it mattered specifically that the stone was lab-grown. Average LGD ring: 1.9ct for $4,600. Average natural ring: 1.6ct for $7,000 — [Business Wire, The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260218045442/en/The-Knot-Worldwide-Unveils-2026-Real-Weddings-Study); [Rapaport](https://rapaport.com/news/lab-grown-taking-over-engagement-ring-spending-the-knot/); [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/lab-diamonds-engagement-market/)
- LGD engagement ring sales rose 31% in 2025, while natural diamond engagement ring sales fell 4%. This comes from a search summary; the underlying source is probably Tenoris or Golan data reported in trade press — [InStore "Lab-Grown Tipping Point"](https://instoremag.com/talking-diamonds-the-lab-grown-tipping-point/)
- Signet: LGD is about 40% of bridal-band sales and about 15% of fashion jewellery revenue, and the fashion share doubled in one year. LGD fashion reached 14% of fashion sales in Q2 FY26 (quarter ended about Aug 2025) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/signet-shines-lab-grown-diamonds-175000939.html); [National Jeweler, Signet Q2](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/14234-lab-grown-diamonds-lower-priced-fashion-jewelry-drive-signet-jewelers-q2-sales)

**Moissanite**
- Market-research estimates of the global moissanite jewellery market in 2025 disagree badly: $0.16B, $0.88B, $1.2B and $1.7B. These are low-quality paid-report aggregators — [Knowledge Sourcing](https://www.knowledge-sourcing.com/report/moissanite-market); [MarketIntelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/moissanite-jewelry-market); [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/moissanite-jewellery-market); [Verified Market Reports](https://www.verifiedmarketreports.com/product/moissanite-market/)
- One aggregator projects moissanite jewellery reaching $1.5B by 2033 (9.9% CAGR, 2026-2033) — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/trend-of-mossanite-jewelry)
- Charles & Colvard (the original moissanite maker):
  - Finished-jewellery sales fell about 20% year on year in the quarter to Mar 31, 2025 ($3.93M vs $4.88M) — [C&C 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000114036125028626/ef20051325_10q.htm)
  - Delisted from Nasdaq in 2025, then filed Chapter 11 in March 2026 ($19.2M in assets, $10.5M in debts). It cited falling LGD and moissanite prices, soaring metal prices and competition — [National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/14761-charles-colvard-files-for-bankruptcy-citing-price-pressures); [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/charles-colvard-chapter-11/)
  - AJS Creations, a New York LGD e-commerce group, won its assets for $2.7M. An earlier $1.5M stalking-horse deal with Van Lang Jewelry had been announced in April 2026 — [National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/14902-charles-colvard-may-sell-assets-for-1-5m); [grown-diamonds.com](https://grown-diamonds.com/what-happened-to-charles-and-colvard-ajs-creations-acquisition/)

### Inferences
- LGD is now the default for US bridal and is spreading fast into fashion and everyday jewellery. Because growth there comes from units rather than price, the fashion category is a volume, price-point game.
- Moissanite has lost its main advantage, a big price gap versus diamond, because LGD prices collapsed. The leading moissanite brand's bankruptcy suggests moissanite should be a low-price add-on or a line for Etsy-style shoppers, not the brand's core.

### Gaps
- There is no clean, analyst-grade split of US LGD jewellery sales in dollars for 2025. Zimnisky's older forecast of about $3.9B global LGD jewellery for 2025 is dated.
- It is unclear whether Tenoris's 42.1% is units or value. The full article could not be fetched.
- There is no reliable US-specific moissanite market size.

## Q2. LGD price deflation, the De Beers Lightbox exit, and what it means for fashion vs bridal

### Takeaway
LGD wholesale prices fell about 26% in 2025, 14% year on year in Q1 2026 and 13% in Q2 2026. The decline is slowing, and prices are near production cost. Retail prices have fallen less, so retailers still earn markups above 80%. De Beers shut Lightbox in 2025 after wholesale LGD prices fell about 90% from its $800/ct launch price. The result: LGD is now a cheap commodity stone whose value comes from design, gold and brand rather than the diamond itself.

### Cited Findings
**Wholesale price declines (Edahn Golan)**
- LGD wholesale prices fell 26% year on year in 2025, the slowest decline since the category emerged. Goods of 1-3ct fell 42% versus Q2 2024. Retailers paid an average $191/ct for a round 1ct IGI-certified LGD, almost double the wholesale market trading price — [Edahn Golan, "Wholesale Prices Down 26% in 2025"](https://www.edahngolan.com/lab-grown-diamond-wholesale-prices-2025/); [Edahn Golan Q2](https://www.edahngolan.com/lab-grown-diamond-wholesale-prices-drop-7-in-q2/)
- Golan's Q1 2026 price list: midstream loose LGD prices fell another 14% year on year. Q2 2026: down about 13% year on year on average — [JCK "Lab-Grown Diamond Wholesale Prices Are in Freefall"](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/lab-grown-wholesale-freefall/)

**Retail prices and margins (Paul Zimnisky)**
- Zimnisky's 2026 retail-margin update (May 2026): retailers still take markups of more than 80% over wholesale on LGD, with only a 1-2 point change between 2025 and 2026 — [JCK Zimnisky](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/paul-zimnisky-on-diamond-market/); [Zimnisky margin chart](https://www.paulzimnisky.com/Lab-grown-Diamond-Retail-Margin-Analysis-Chart); [Zimnisky data](https://www.paulzimnisky.com/data)
- A search summary attributed these to Zimnisky: a 1ct natural diamond averages about $4,500-6,500 at US retail, versus about $446-678 for a 1ct lab stone bought direct. It also cited a 3ct D VVS lab round at $126/ct in Q1 2026. **Treat both with caution.** They likely come from retailer blogs such as Draco Diamond, not from Zimnisky directly. A 3ct D VVS at $126/ct looks implausibly low for retail and may be a wholesale or index figure — [Draco Diamond price trend](https://www.dracodiamond.com/blogs/news/lab-diamond-price-trend-report-2020-2026); [Velur](https://velur.de/en/blog/lab-diamant-preisentwicklung-2026/)
- Search summary: 1ct LGD at about $750-1,000, down 74% from 2020 levels. This is a secondary source — [Gem Guide April 2025](https://files.gemguide.com/20250401062337/2025_AprilLGD.pdf)
- AIDI, an industry association blog, argues LGD prices will hold in 2026 and that the "74%" wholesale decline is a margin shift that retailers are not disclosing. This is an advocacy source — [AIDI](https://aidi.org/74-percent-lab-grown-wholesale-prices-margin-migration-disclosure/); [AIDI price stability](https://aidi.org/lab-grown-diamond-price-stability-2026/)

**De Beers Lightbox**
- In May 2025, De Beers announced it would close Lightbox, launched in 2018 at $800/ct fixed pricing. LGD jewellery prices had "fallen 90% at wholesale" since then. Consumer-facing activity stopped in summer 2025, and the factory closed by the end of 2025. Element Six now does industrial synthetics only — [De Beers Group press release, 2025](https://www.debeersgroup.com/news-insights/latest-group-news/2025/de-beers-group-announces-intention-to-close-lightbox-business); [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/de-beers-shutting-lightbox/); [Rapaport](https://rapaport.com/news/de-beers-to-close-lightbox-lab-grown-diamond-brand/)

**Where the demand is**
- Consumers are moving to larger LGD stones: the Knot average is 1.9ct, and Tenoris reports interest in 2ct stones — [Tenoris "Larger Lab-Grown Diamonds"](https://www.tenoris.bi/consumers-show-growing-appetite-for-larger-lab-grown-diamonds/)

### Inferences
- With a 1ct LGD at under about $200/ct wholesale, the gold is often the biggest cost in a fashion piece.
- For studs, tennis bracelets, pendants and bands, the price consumers perceive depends on design and brand trust, not carat price. Deflation helps fashion and self-purchase (bigger, more sparkly pieces at the same price) but hurts resale and "investment" messaging, which is where natural diamonds position themselves.
- Slower deflation in 2026 lowers the risk of holding inventory. Even so, a D2C brand should avoid claiming LGD holds its value.

### Gaps
- There is no fashion-specific LGD retail price index. The notes above do not break down melee (small stone) or pointer pricing.
- The Zimnisky figures could not be verified directly (fetch blocked).

## Q3. Demographics and demand: Gen Z and millennials, self-purchase, everyday fine jewellery, and the 10K/14K shift driven by gold prices

### Takeaway
Self-purchasing and value-seeking younger buyers are driving demand for everyday LGD fine jewellery. Gold hit a record of about $5,595/oz in January 2026 and was about $4,250-4,490/oz in September 2026. That has pushed metal costs up, cut into brand margins, and moved demand toward 14K and 10K gold and lighter designs.

### Cited Findings
**Buyers**
- BriteCo survey: 3 in 4 Gen Z adults are more likely to buy fine jewellery for themselves than wait for it as a gift — [Eastern Progress/BriteCo](https://www.easternprogress.com/americans-are-embracing-self-gifting-fine-jewelry/article_02233b47-aff8-5be0-8174-494d4f97f686.html)
- De Beers 2026 Diamond Report: Gen Z made up 23% of natural diamond demand by value in 2025 while being 18% of consumers. So Gen Z does not buy only LGD. This is a secondary summary — [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/gen-z-natural-diamond-demand-us-market-trends-2026/)
- The claim that more than 70% of millennials prefer LGD comes from an Indian LGD retailer's blog. It is low quality and unverified — [Varniya](https://varniya.com/blogs/labgrown-diamond-news/what-percent-of-millenials-are-buying-lab-grown-diamond-rings)

**Brand evidence of demand**
- Brilliant Earth Q2 2026: fine jewellery bookings grew 32% year on year and reached 18% of total bookings. Fine jewellery above $500 grew more than 40%. Total orders fell 2.1%, while orders above $500 grew 5% — [Yahoo Finance, BRLT Q2 2026 call](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/brilliant-earth-group-inc-brlt-231300136.html); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/brilliant-earth-q2-2026-slides-premium-shift-drives-margin-gains-93CH-4842735)

**Gold prices**
- Record high of $5,595.42/oz on Jan 29, 2026 — [search summary citing Forever Rox / MyGoldCalc](https://foreverrox.com/blogs/rox-reports/gold-4350-record-high-what-it-means-for-your-jewelry)
- Gold was about $4,350/oz on June 16, 2026 — [Forever Rox](https://foreverrox.com/blogs/rox-reports/gold-4350-record-high-what-it-means-for-your-jewelry)
- In September 2026 gold was $4,491 (Sep 3), $4,378 (Sep 20) and $4,251 (Sep 24). That is down about 8.7% over the month but up about 13% year on year — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/current-price-of-gold-09-03-2026/); [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold)
- 14K gold was about $82-89/g in May-September 2026 — [MyGoldCalc](https://mygoldcalc.com/gold-price/2026/09)

**Effect on brands and purity choice**
- Brilliant Earth's Q2 2026 gross margin of 57.9% was down 40bp year on year, which it attributed to "high metal costs and tariffs" — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/brilliant-earth-group-inc-brlt-231300136.html)
- Charles & Colvard cited "skyrocketing precious metal prices" as a cause of its bankruptcy — [National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/14761-charles-colvard-files-for-bankruptcy-citing-price-pressures)
- 14K is the most popular purity in the US. 10K is 41.7% pure gold and 14K is 58.3%. These are retailer explainers — [Angara blog](https://www.angara.com/blog/10k-vs-14k-gold/); [Von Diamonds](https://vondiamonds.com/blogs/news/10k-gold-vs-14k-gold-jewelry)
- One claim says 14K gold chain sales grew 38% from Q4 2025 to Q1 2026. **Its source is unclear (a crypto or news aggregator) and it is not verified** — [MEXC news](https://www.mexc.com/news/372812)

### Inferences
- At roughly $4,300/oz, a 2g 14K pendant or stud pair has about $165-180 of gold at spot, before making charges and loss. Gold is now the main cost driver.
- A D2C brand should design light (hollow or thin), offer 10K entry price points and 14K as the core, and consider pricing tied to the gold price.

### Gaps
- There are no hard industry statistics (Tenoris, Circana) on the US sales mix of 10K vs 14K vs 18K in 2025-26. The trade-press coverage of this shift could not be fetched.

## Q4. US D2C and mass competitors: price points and positioning

### Takeaway
The incumbents split two ways:
- Bridal-led LGD e-tailers (Brilliant Earth, Grown Brilliance/Clean Origin, Frank Darling) are adding fine jewellery.
- Fashion-led D2C brands (Mejuri, Aurate, Ana Luisa) sell LGD-set gold pieces at about $150-1,000.

Mass retailers (Signet's Kay/Zales/Jared, Pandora) have pushed LGD into fashion, with mixed results: Pandora's LGD line is only about 1% of revenue.

## Q5. Indian-origin brands and exporters selling D2C to the US; India's share of US LGD and jewellery imports; tariffs

### Takeaway
India is the largest cutter and exporter of LGD: about 36% of global LGD exports in 2025. But the US share of India's LGD exports fell sharply, from about 63% in 2022 to about 17% in January-May 2026. Tariffs peaked at 50% (August 2025) before the February 2026 interim deal set them at 18% for LGD and finished jewellery. India's big LGD brands (Limelight, Solitario, Jewelbox) are focused on India, not US D2C. The Indian D2C presence in the US is mostly Etsy sellers, plus Angara, a US-headquartered brand with Indian roots.

### Cited Findings
**Trade flows**
- The US share of India's LGD shipments fell from 63.49% (2022) to 16.66% (January-May 2026). India's share of global LGD exports rose from 28.03% (2022) to 35.73% (2025) — [Business Standard, Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/india-s-natural-diamond-exports-decline-amid-rise-of-lab-grown-gems-126081400834_1.html); [Business Standard Substack](https://businessstandard.substack.com/p/indias-diamond-exports-lose-lustre)
- India's LGD exports have overtaken natural diamonds by volume — [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/indias-lab-grown-diamond-exports-surpass-naturals-by-volume/)

**Tariff timeline**
- July-August 2025: the US imposed a 25% reciprocal tariff on India, then doubled it to 50% on Aug 27, 2025. This put roughly $10B a year of Indian gem and jewellery exports at risk. Cut-and-polished diamond exports were forecast to fall 17-20% in FY26 — [GJEPC Solitaire](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/the-50-shock-trumps-tariff-blitz-puts-10-billion-trade-in-peril/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/us-tariffs-hit-diamond-exports-india-cut-polished-shipments-fy26-125092200697_1.html)
- Feb 7, 2026: the US-India interim framework cut the reciprocal tariff to 18%:
  - Finished diamond jewellery, coloured-gem jewellery and **lab-grown diamonds: 18%**
  - Loose natural diamonds and coloured gemstones: 0%

  — [White House joint statement, Feb 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/02/united-states-india-joint-statement); [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/us-india-deal-jewelry-gems/); [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/india-us-trade-deal-18-percent-reciprocal-tariffs-items-list-gems-and-jewellery-home-decor-apparel-toys-machinery-and-parts-515062-2026-02-07); [GJEPC Solitaire](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/a-new-dawn-for-india-us-trade-zero-duty-framework-to-revitalise-gem-jewellery-sector/); [Rapaport](https://rapaport.com/news/us-to-nix-tariff-on-indian-gems-and-natural-diamonds/)
- India's gem and jewellery exports were $23.19B in April-January FY26 — [Indian Jeweller](https://www.indianjeweller.in/Indian-Jewellery-News/15983/india-gem-jewellery-exports-at-2319-billion-in-apriljanuary-indiaus-trade-framework-restores-18-tariff-access)

### Inferences
- An India-based D2C brand shipping finished LGD gold jewellery to the US currently faces an 18% reciprocal tariff plus the normal MFN jewellery duty (about 5.5-6.5% for gold jewellery; not verified here).
- The US de minimis exemption ended for all countries in August 2025, so duty must be priced into landed cost. This was not verified in these notes and should be checked by the tariffs/logistics researcher.
- The drop in the US share of India's LGD exports means Indian loose-stone exporters are diversifying. That could leave Surat supply plentiful and cheap for a finished-goods brand.
