# Cross-border Logistics, Insurance, Returns & Payments: India-based D2C jewellery brand selling to US consumers (notes as of 2026-09-25)

Research note: many primary sites (fedex.com, docs.stripe.com, shiprocket.in, taxguru.in, gjepc.org, clickpost.ai, instoremag.com) were blocked from direct fetching in this research environment, so several findings below rely on search-result snippets of those pages. Treat numbers as "verify before relying on them". URLs are given so the writer or reader can check them.

## 1. Shipping options, costs, transit and DDP after de minimis ended / India Post suspension

### Takeaway
Express integrators (FedEx/UPS/DHL) will carry jewellery India→US, but their standard jewellery liability is very low ($500–$1,000). Real cover for high-value pieces comes from contract programmes (FedEx Declared Value Advantage, up to $25k international) or from specialist valuables carriers and insurers (Malca-Amit, Brink's, Parcel Pro). The US ended de minimis for all origins on 29 Aug 2025, so every parcel now pays duty, and DDP (the seller prepays duty) is the practical default for D2C. India Post suspended US mail on 22–25 Aug 2025 and **resumed all categories on 15 Oct 2025** with a DDP product.

### Cited Findings
**De minimis end and the India Post suspension**
- US Executive Order 14324 (30 Jul 2025) withdrew de minimis treatment (the duty-free exemption for goods up to USD 800), effective 29 Aug 2025. All postal items to the US are now dutiable whatever their value, except gifts up to USD 100. — [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/india-suspends-most-postal-services-to-us-amid-new-customs-duty-rules/); [AIR/NewsOnAir, Aug 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-post-temporarily-halts-postal-services-to-us-from-august-25)
- India Post suspended most postal services to the US from late Aug 2025 (reported as 22 and 25 Aug). The reason was the new CBP rules on collecting and remitting import duty. — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/india-post-suspends-all-mail-services-to-us-in-absence-of-rule-clarity); [NewsOnAir](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-post-temporarily-halts-postal-services-to-us-from-august-25)
- The Department of Posts resumed **all categories** of international postal services to the USA from **15 Oct 2025**, after putting in place a Delivery Duty Paid (DDP) mechanism. — [NewsOnAir, 15 Oct 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-resumes-international-postal-services-to-us-after-suspension); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/india-to-resume-postal-services-to-us-from-october-15)
- How India Post DDP works: the customer pays all applicable US import duties at booking in India. The duties are remitted to CBP through authorised "Qualified Parties". — [GJEPC Solitaire (via search snippet)](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/india-post-resumes-all-postal-services-to-the-us-launches-ddp-product-with-6-5-lower-duty-on-jewellery/)
- At the time of resumption (Oct 2025), postal shipments from India to the US paid a **flat 50% duty on declared FOB value**. The base MFN jewellery duty (about 6.5%) was not stacked on top, so GJEPC called post "6.5% cheaper" for jewellery than courier. — [NewsOnAir](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-resumes-international-postal-services-to-us-after-suspension); [GJEPC Solitaire headline/snippet](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/india-post-resumes-all-postal-services-to-the-us-launches-ddp-product-with-6-5-lower-duty-on-jewellery/)

**Tariff context in 2026 (affects the DDP amount; the tariff researcher should confirm)**
- From 24 Jul 2026 the US replaced the temporary Section 122 surcharge with a Section 301 regime. India sits in the **10% band**, which applies to natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds, coloured gemstones and jewellery. Jewellery still pays MFN duty of 5.5–6% as well, for an effective duty of **about 15.5–16%**. — [GJEPC Solitaire](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/us-retains-10-tariff-on-indian-exports-under-new-section-301-regime/); [The Week, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/07/24/us-tariffs-section-301-india.html); [JewelBuzz](https://jewelbuzz.in/us-retains-10-tariff-on-indian-exports-under-new-section-301-regime/)
- Conflicting or stale data: some search snippets mention an "additional 25% on Indian gold jewellery from Aug 27", making 50% in total. That refers to the **2025** IEEPA tariffs, not 2026. — [Tribune India (50% tariffs)](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/gold-jewellery-sector-raise-alarms-over-50-us-tariffs/amp)

**Express carriers: jewellery policies and value limits**
- FedEx standard jewellery shipments: maximum liability and declared value of **$1,000**. — [FedEx declared value page (via Shipware / search snippet)](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/declared-value.html); [Shipware](https://shipware.com/blog/fedex-shipping-insurance-a-comprehensive-guide/)
- FedEx **Declared Value Advantage** (jewellery, watches, components) allows up to **$100,000 per domestic shipment and $25,000 to select international destinations**. It is contract-only, for US business account holders shipping jewellery 2–3 times a week. An India-origin shipper would not qualify directly. — [FedEx High-Value Jewelry DVA](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/high-value-jewelry-declared-value-form.html); [FedEx Jewelry Shipping Program PDF](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/us-united-states/services/FedEx_Jewelry_Shipping_Program.pdf)
- FedEx prohibits commodities worth over US$20,000 without approval. — [Speed Commerce / FedEx summary](https://www.speedcommerce.com/insights/how-to-ship-high-value-items-the-10-best-tips/)
- UPS caps jewellery at **$500 maximum declared value per package**. The workaround is Parcel Pro, which UPS owns. — [Shippo 2026 guide](https://goshippo.com/blog/how-to-ship-jewelry-for-your-e-commerce-store-tips-examples-for-shipping-jewelry)
- DHL Express and UPS India both offer DDP and DDU (Delivery Duty Unpaid) India→US. — [DHL India DDP](https://www.dhl.com/in-en/home/ship/ddp-shipping.html); [UPS India customs](https://www.ups.com/in/en/support/international-tools-resources/understanding-customs)
- Indicative express cost India→US: FedEx/DHL/UPS **$62–$74.50 per kg**, with 2–6 day transit to Tier-1 US metros. This comes from an aggregator blog, not carrier tariffs. — [ClickPost 2026](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/courier-charges-for-usa)

**Aggregators**
- Shiprocket X offers a DDP product for US shipments that prepays duties, tariffs and taxes. It also publishes a jewellery-to-USA export guide listing the documents needed: IEC, GSTIN, PAN, BIS hallmark, GJEPC membership, and insurance. — [Shiprocket X DDP](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/delivery-duty-paid-ddp-usa-shipments/); [Shiprocket X jewellery guide](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/export-jewellery-india-to-usa-guide/)

**Specialist valuables carriers**
- Malca-Amit offers secure door-to-door delivery and insurance for diamonds and jewellery worldwide, and operates in India with a US division in New York. It can take full liability in transit, 100% underwritten by Lloyd's, or limited liability if the shipper already has cover. Cover can be bought per shipment or annually. Services include express shipping, bonded warehousing and jewellery block insurance. — [Malca-Amit secured delivery](https://www.malca-amit.com/diamonds-jewellery/secured-delivery); [Malca-Amit express](https://www.malca-amit.com/diamonds-jewellery/express-shipping); [Malca-Amit home](https://www.malca-amit.com/)
- Brink's Global Services covers the diamond and jewellery industry and e-commerce platforms, "combining security, insurance, and simplicity to move lower-value parcels" (Brink's Connect), across more than 100 countries. — [Brink's Connect](https://connect.brinks.com/); [Brink's Diamonds & Jewelry](https://brinksglobal.com/diamonds-and-jewelry)
- Ferrari Group is a valuables logistics firm that IPO'd in Feb 2025 and has an India office. It has said it is expanding in India. — [Ferrari Group India office](https://www.ferrarigroup.net/office-locator/asia-pacific/india/); [Moats & Markets](https://moatsandmarkets.com/ferrari-group-plc/)

**Indian export compliance for courier jewellery**
- CBIC has a dedicated framework for e-commerce exports of jewellery by courier, covering the courier shipping bill and the rules for returns. — [TaxGuru: framework](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/regulatory-framework-e-commerce-exports-jewellery-courier-mode.html); [TaxGuru: circular](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/e-commerce-exports-jewellery-courier-mode-reg.html)

### Inferences
- For pieces worth roughly $500–$3,000, the realistic route is express courier (FedEx/UPS/DHL via an aggregator like Shiprocket X) under DDP, with separate third-party insurance. The carriers' own declared-value cover does not reach these values for an Indian shipper. Above roughly $5k–$10k, Malca-Amit or Brink's become more cost-effective per unit of risk.
- The postal DDP route was cheaper under the 2025 flat-50% regime. Whether the postal flat rate still applies after the 2026 tariff changes (Section 122, then Section 301 at 10%) is unconfirmed. It matters a lot for landed cost.
- Low declared values set to cut duty are customs fraud, and they also void insurance recovery. DDP pricing therefore needs about 16% duty plus brokerage built in.

### Gaps
- No current (2026) published India→US rate cards were found for Malca-Amit, Brink's Connect or Ferrari Group. These are quote-based.
- India Post's per-parcel value cap for jewellery exports and the current (post-July-2026) postal duty basis to the US were not confirmed.
- DHL Express's specific jewellery declared-value limit from India was not found.
- Shiprocket X's exact jewellery value cap and insurance limit were not retrievable (site blocked).

## 2. Shipment insurance, and whether carriers cover jewellery

### Takeaway
Carrier liability for jewellery is capped low ($500 at UPS, $1,000 at FedEx standard) or restricted. Brands insure through specialist products: Parcel Pro (a UPS Capital subsidiary), Malca-Amit (Lloyd's-backed), jewellers' block policies (Jewelers Mutual partners with Malca-Amit), or shipping insurers such as Shipsurance, whose caps depend on service.

### Cited Findings
- Parcel Pro is a UPS subsidiary that specialises in insured shipping for jewellery, loose stones, watches and precious metals. It offers cover up to **$200K** to and from select countries with pre-approval (US market) and up to **$100K** internationally (per the UK site). — [Parcel Pro US](https://www.parcelpro.com/us/en/insured-shipping.html); [Parcel Pro Select](https://www.parcelpro.com/us/en/insured-shipping/parcel-pro-select.html); [UPS Parcel Pro](https://www.ups.com/us/en/ups-parcelpro); [Parcel Pro GB](https://www.parcelpro.com/gb/en/home.html)
- UPS Capital markets Parcel Pro Select in Asian markets (Thailand, Hong Kong pages exist). — [UPS Capital TH](https://upscapital.com/th-en/supply-chain-protection/parcel-pro-select/)
- Jewelers Mutual promotes jewellery transport and storage through its Malca-Amit partnership. — [Jewelers Mutual](https://www.jewelersmutual.com/business/business-risk-management/storage-transportation)
- Shipsurance cover through ShippingEasy varies by service. The examples cited are US domestic USPS limits (First Class $999.99, Priority up to $10,000). No jewellery-specific terms were found. — [ShippingEasy](https://shippingeasy.com/blog/how-to-ship-jewelry/)
- If you declare a value on prohibited items and they are lost, you are not reimbursed. — [FedEx declared value (search snippet)](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/declared-value.html)

### Inferences
- Parcel Pro's availability for **India-origin** shippers is unconfirmed. Its UPS Capital Asia pages suggest regional availability, so ask for a quote.
- A brand-level annual jewellers' block or marine-transit policy from an Indian insurer (not researched here) may be cheaper than per-parcel cover at volume.

### Gaps
- Premium rates (for example, cost per $100 insured) for Parcel Pro, Malca-Amit or Shipsurance on India→US jewellery lanes were not found.
- Whether Shipsurance covers jewellery on international lanes, or excludes it, is unconfirmed.

## 3. Model alternatives: direct per-order shipping vs bulk export to a US 3PL / importer of record

### Takeaway
Bulk export to a US 3PL needs a US entity (or appointed importer of record), formal customs entry and a jewellery-capable secure 3PL. In return it gives 2–5 day domestic delivery, US returns and a one-time duty on bulk value. Direct per-order DDP needs no US entity but carries per-parcel brokerage and friction.

### Cited Findings
- Indian brands need a US entity (LLC), an importer of record (IOR) and customs clearance to store goods in the US. Options include a 3PL, Amazon FBA or leased space. — [Bluebonnet Supplies guide](https://feeds.bluebonnetsupplies.com/blog/set-up-us-warehouse-india-ecommerce); [Bluebonnet 3PL](https://feeds.bluebonnetsupplies.com/blog/3pl-warehouse-usa-indian-manufacturers)
- Some US 3PLs (for example InSync Fulfillment) provide IOR services for international brands. — [search summary of 3PL results](https://www.fulfill.com/top-3pl-companies)
- Jewellery-specialist US 3PLs include Buske Logistics and Speed Commerce, which offer secure handling and multi-checkpoint verification. — [Buske](https://www.buske.com/industries-served/jewelry-products); [Speed Commerce](https://www.speedcommerce.com/industries-we-serve/jewelry-products/)
- Malca-Amit also offers bonded warehousing (goods held without duty until released) as an alternative. — [Malca-Amit](https://www.malca-amit.com/)
- One source claims direct-to-consumer parcels are more likely to be flagged or detained than bulk shipments into a licensed facility. This is a vendor-blog claim, low confidence. — [Bluebonnet Supplies](https://feeds.bluebonnetsupplies.com/blog/set-up-us-warehouse-india-ecommerce)

### Inferences
- Inventory placed in the US through a US entity creates US tax exposure (see section 6) and sales-tax nexus in the warehouse state. Consignment stock held by a US 3PL still requires the Indian exporter to realise export proceeds within FEMA timelines (15 months).
- Hybrid model: keep bestseller SKUs in a US 3PL and ship made-to-order or high-value pieces direct via DDP.

### Gaps
- No published 2026 pricing for jewellery 3PLs (storage, pick/pack, vault fees) or US IOR-as-a-service fees was found.
- No sources were found on consignment stock arrangements specifically for Indian lab-grown diamond brands.

## 4. Returns from the US to India

### Takeaway
Returned jewellery can be re-imported into India duty-free under Notification 45/2017-Customs, amended on 30 Mar 2026. Courier jewellery e-commerce returns have specific identity conditions. Many brands avoid round trips by consolidating returns at a US address or 3PL.

### Cited Findings
- Duty-free re-import under Notification 45/2017-Cus applies to goods returned after export for genuine commercial reasons. All export incentives claimed (for example drawback or RoDTEP) must be reversed, and the exemption must be claimed on the bill of entry with documents linking it to the original export. — [TaxTMI guide](https://www.taxtmi.com/article/detailed?id=15669); [TaxTMI notification](https://www.taxtmi.com/notifications?id=120395)
- Notification **08/2026-Customs (30 Mar 2026)** amended 45/2017 clause (d) to clarify that the exemption applies only where the re-imported goods are the same as those originally exported. — [search snippet; TaxGuru amendment](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/cbic-notifies-amendment-to-notification-no-45-2017-cus-dated-30-06-2017.html)
- E-commerce return rates of 20–25% are cited, against under 5% for conventional exports. — [TaxGuru jewellery courier framework](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/regulatory-framework-e-commerce-exports-jewellery-courier-mode.html)
- Under CBIC's courier jewellery framework, a return must be initiated by the **same consignee** on the **same e-commerce platform** as the original courier shipping bill. Alternatively, a courier or logistics provider can return goods that failed import clearance abroad. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/regulatory-framework-e-commerce-exports-jewellery-courier-mode.html); [TaxGuru circular](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/e-commerce-exports-jewellery-courier-mode-reg.html)
- Overview of re-import and re-export under the Customs Act 1962. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/custom-duty/re-importation-re-exportation-goods-under-customs-act-1962.html)

### Inferences
- US duty paid on a DDP shipment is generally lost when the item is returned, unless a US duty drawback is claimed, which is complex for small parcels. Return shipping India-bound plus insurance may cost $40–$80 or more per parcel at express rates. US returns consolidation (a 3PL or returns-address service) with periodic bulk re-export to India is usually cheaper.
- Signifyd flags return fraud in jewellery, such as swapped stones or returned fakes. Returns need identity verification: laser inscriptions and certificates for lab-grown diamonds and moissanite.

### Gaps
- No sources were retrieved on specific US returns-address services (for example ReturnGO, Returnly, or 3PL returns desks) or their pricing for India-bound jewellery.
- The time limit for re-import under the courier jewellery circular was not confirmed.

## 5. Payments: gateways, FEMA/FIRC/e-BRC, fees, chargebacks, fraud and BNPL

### Takeaway
Shopify Payments is **not available** to India-entity merchants. The main routes are RBI-regulated gateways (Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree: about 3% plus 18% GST on the fee), PayPal (about 4.4% + $0.30, plus 3–4% FX markup), or Stripe India (invite-only, IEC required). Collection accounts such as Skydo, Wise and Payoneer are mainly for B2B invoices, not card checkout. Jewellery is a high-fraud vertical, and chargeback protection matters.

### Cited Findings
**Gateways**
- Shopify Payments is available in 23+ countries but **not India** (2026). Indian merchants must use a third-party gateway. — [Razorpay 2026 guide](https://razorpay.com/blog/setting-up-international-card-payments-on-shopify-india-a-step-by-step-razorpay-integration-guide-2026/); [Ecosire 2026](https://ecosire.com/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-by-country-2026)
- Razorpay charges about **3% per international card transaction + 18% GST** on the fee in its export flow, settling in INR. — [Razorpay Shopify cost guide](https://razorpay.com/blog/shopify-payment-gateway-cost-india/)
- Stripe India is **invite-only**. For international payments the account must be a registered Indian business (proprietorship, LLP or company, not an individual) and, for physical goods, must hold a valid **IEC**. It supports 135+ currencies. — [Stripe invite-only](https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-accounts-are-invite-only-in-india); [Stripe India FAQ](https://support.stripe.com/questions/india-faq); [Stripe India international payments docs](https://docs.stripe.com/india-accept-international-payments); [Stripe cross-border pricing](https://support.stripe.com/questions/additional-pricing-information-for-indian-cross-border-sellers)
- PayPal India charges **4.4% + $0.30** per international payment, plus a 3–4% FX markup and 18% GST on fees, for an effective cost of about 5–8%. It issues a monthly FIRA. Merchant-rate discounts must be applied for. — [PayPal IN fees](https://www.paypal.com/in/business/paypal-business-fees); [Skydo on PayPal](https://www.skydo.com/compare/paypal-pricing); [Xflow](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/paypal-transaction-fees)
- Skydo charges a flat $19–$29 per payment (0.3% above $10k), with free per-transaction FIRA and eBRC support. — [Skydo vs Wise](https://www.skydo.com/compare/skydo-vs-wise); [Xflow on Skydo vs Wise](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/skydo-vs-wise)
- Wise India costs about 1.6–1.8% for conversion, plus $2 per FIRA and GST on the fee. It cannot hold USD balances long-term. — [Skydo on Wise pricing](https://www.skydo.com/compare/wise-pricing)
- Payoneer supplies FIRA/FIRS documents through a partner bank. — [Payoneer FIRC guide](https://www.payoneer.com/resources/news-events/decoding-firc-a-guide-for-indian-exporters-of-goods-services/)

**FEMA compliance**
- Export proceeds must be realised within 15 months. The **FEMA (Export and Import) Regulations, 2026 take effect 1 Oct 2026**, with 15 months (18 months for INR-invoiced exports). — [Razorpay on 2026 rules](https://razorpay.com/blog/realisation-repatriation-export-proceeds-rules/); [Razorpay RBI circular](https://razorpay.com/blog/rbi-circular-on-export-realisation/); [Skydo](https://www.skydo.com/blog/realisation-and-repatriation)
- DGFT issues the e-BRC once payment is reconciled against the export. Banks charge about ₹200 per FIRC, with FX markups of 1–3.5%. — [Winvesta](https://www.winvesta.in/blog/businesses/firc-vs-fira-vs-e-brc-which-proof-of-foreign-payment-do-you-actually-need); [Skydo on FEMA](https://www.skydo.com/blog/fema-foreign-exchange-management-act)

**Fraud and chargebacks**
- High-value goods like jewellery are frequent fraud targets. The fraud rate on transactions over $500 was **22x higher** than on transactions under $100. — [Signifyd industry fraud stats](https://www.signifyd.com/blog/ecommerce-fraud-eight-industries/)
- A fraudulent $100 purchase costs merchants $196.20 in total (Signifyd). Merchants lose an average $3.75 per $1 of chargebacks. — [Signifyd chargeback guide](https://www.signifyd.com/resources/fraud-101/chargeback-fraud-complete-guide-for-merchants/); [Signifyd chargeback rate](https://www.signifyd.com/blog/chargeback-rate-calculation/)
- Signifyd offers guaranteed fraud protection, reimbursing fraud chargebacks on orders it approved, and automated representment. — [Signifyd guarantee](https://www.signifyd.com/guaranteed-fraud-protection/); [State of Fraud & Returns 2025](https://www.signifyd.com/ecommerce-fraud-and-returns-trends-2025/)
- Industry chargeback benchmarks. — [Swipesum](https://www.swipesum.com/insights/chargeback-rate-by-industry-and-business-type)

**BNPL**
- Shop Pay Installments (powered by Affirm in the US) is available only to store owners in the **US, Canada and UK**, for $35–$30,000. — [Shopify BNPL blog](https://www.shopify.com/blog/buy-now-pay-later)
- Klarna requires a local business registration, currency and bank account for each region it serves. — [Shopify community](https://community.shopify.com/t/are-there-any-buy-now-pay-later-options-in-uk/202357); [Klarna Shopify](https://www.klarna.com/international/enterprise/platforms-and-partners/shopify/)

### Inferences
- An India-only entity effectively loses US BNPL (Affirm, Shop Pay Installments, most likely Klarna US) and Shopify Payments or Shopify Protect. That is a real conversion handicap on $1k+ jewellery tickets and a strong argument for a US entity.
- Indian gateways (Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree) settle in INR and generate the export documentation (for example the OPGSP route, where a payment aggregator handles cross-border collection), which simplifies FEMA and e-BRC. However, US cardholders may see higher decline rates on foreign-acquired transactions (not sourced; flag as a hypothesis).

### Gaps
- No jewellery-specific chargeback-rate statistic was found. Rates of 0.5–1%+ are commonly cited but were not sourced here.
- Cashfree and PayU international pricing and payout times were not verified.
- It is unconfirmed whether Shopify Protect or Shopify fraud tools are usable without Shopify Payments.

## 6. US LLC or entity option for Indian founders

### Takeaway
A Delaware or Wyoming LLC (Stripe Atlas $500 for Delaware; DIY Wyoming about $100–150) unlocks Shopify Payments, Stripe US, PayPal US and BNPL. US inventory, a 3PL or US staff can create effectively connected income (ECI) and state nexus. Economic sales-tax nexus (typically $100k per state) applies to remote sellers regardless of entity.

### Cited Findings
- Atlas costs $500 for a Delaware LLC or C-corp. A DIY Wyoming LLC costs about $100–150. Stripe and PayPal are easier to access with a US LLC. — [Tax Haven Directory](https://taxhavendirectory.com/blog/stripe-atlas-vs-us-llc-non-residents); [BusinessAnywhere 2026 guide](https://businessanywhere.io/us-llc-guide-indian-founders-us-business/)
- Stripe Atlas is invite-only for Indian founders (per a 2026 third-party guide). — [GlobalSolo 2026](https://www.globalsolo.global/blog/stripe-atlas-indian-founders-invite-only-guide-2026)
- An LLC with no US-ECI can owe $0 US federal tax, while a C-corp pays a flat 21%. Required filings include Form 5472 and state fees. On the India side: Schedule FA, LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme), and advance tax on worldwide income. — [Tax Haven Directory](https://taxhavendirectory.com/blog/stripe-atlas-vs-us-llc-non-residents); [Stripe Atlas business taxes](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/business-taxes)
- Economic nexus triggers from sales volume alone, typically $100,000 per state. Many states have dropped transaction-count thresholds. — [Stripe nexus guide](https://stripe.com/resources/more/sales-tax-nexus-laws); [SK Associates 2026](https://www.skassociatesglobal.com/2026/05/sales-tax-nexus-guide-2026-for-amazon.html?m=1)
- GST vs US sales tax differences for Indian founders. — [USAIndiaCFO](https://usaindiacfo.com/gst-vs-us-sales-tax-guide-for-indian-founders/)

### Inferences
- A US LLC selling inventory held in a US 3PL, and fulfilling it there, likely has ECI (US trade or business). A C-corp may then be cleaner than a foreign-owned single-member LLC. This needs tax advice. An Indian parent owning a US sub requires ODI (overseas direct investment) filings under FEMA (not sourced here).
- Common structure: the Indian manufacturer exports at arm's-length transfer price to the US entity, which acts as IOR and sells D2C through Shopify Payments, BNPL and Signifyd. This requires a US bank account (Mercury or Relay are common; not sourced) and ongoing transfer-pricing documentation.

### Gaps
- Current BOI (beneficial ownership) reporting status for foreign-owned LLCs in 2026 was not verified.
- The ODI/FEMA process and cost for an Indian company or founder setting up a US subsidiary was not researched in depth.
- US bank options for non-resident founders (Mercury, Relay) were not verified for 2026.
