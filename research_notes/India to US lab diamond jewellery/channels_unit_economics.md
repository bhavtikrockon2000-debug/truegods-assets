# Go-to-Market Channels, CAC, Pricing and Unit Economics: D2C Gold + Lab-Grown Diamond / Moissanite Jewellery (India to US), 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-09-25. Method note: many primary pages (sec.gov, varos.com, triplewhale.com, jckonline.com, branvas.com, tariffstool.com, movargo.com) were blocked by the research environment's network proxy, so several figures below are taken from search-engine snippets of those pages rather than full-page reads. They are flagged "(snippet)" where this matters. Verify key numbers before relying on them in a financial model.

## 1. Channel options and fees (Shopify, Etsy, Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, Faire)

### Takeaway
Etsy is the lowest-friction launch channel for an India-based seller (~10-11% base fees plus 12-15% Offsite Ads). Amazon fine jewellery is expensive to enter ($5,000 non-refundable application, 20% referral on the first $250). TikTok Shop and Amazon both put fine jewellery behind gating. A Shopify own-store is essential long term, but an Indian entity cannot use Shopify Payments, which pushes founders toward a US entity or third-party gateways that carry an extra 2% Shopify surcharge on the Basic plan.

### Cited Findings
**Shopify**
- 2026 plans: Starter $5/mo, Basic $39/mo, Grow $105/mo, Advanced $399/mo, Plus from $2,300/mo, plus a new "Agentic" plan at $0/mo. Annual billing saves 25% on Basic through Advanced. — [Swell, Shopify Pricing 2026](https://www.swell.is/content/shopify-pricing); [Craftshift](https://craftshift.com/shopify-pricing-plans-2026-complete-breakdown/)
- Shopify Payments online card rates: Basic 2.9% + 30¢, Grow 2.7% + 30¢, Advanced 2.5% + 30¢. Third-party gateway surcharge: 2% (Basic), 1% (Grow), 0.6% (Advanced), 0.2% (Plus). International cards +1.0%; currency conversion 1.5% (reportedly charged on the gross order from April 6, 2026); $15 chargeback fee. — [Swell](https://www.swell.is/content/shopify-pricing); [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/compare/how-much-does-shopify-payments-cost)
- "Most merchants pay 2-3x their base plan price" once apps, themes and fees are included (vendor blog opinion). — [Swell](https://www.swell.is/content/shopify-pricing)
- India-incorporated stores cannot activate Shopify Payments and must use a third-party gateway. For Indian founders with a Delaware C-Corp, Stripe integrates natively, but it requires a US entity, a US bank account and FEMA ODI compliance. Community posts report that Shopify Payments can require an SSN even for a US LLC. — [Playto blog 2026](https://www.playto.so/blogs/how-indian-shopify-merchants-accept-international-payments-2026); [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/cant-activate-shopify-payments-without-ssn/254670); [HynoGo](https://hynogo.com/blog/shopify-payments-outside-us-non-resident)

**Etsy**
- Fees: $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee (on the total including shipping), and US payment processing of 3% + $0.25. Offsite Ads is 15% of attributed sales for shops under $10k trailing-12-month revenue (opt-out allowed) and 12% and mandatory above $10k. Effective mandatory fee rate is typically 11-15% before Offsite Ads. — [Etsy Fees & Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/); [Craftybase 2026](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [Printify 2026](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale/)
- Note: sellers in some countries, including India, may pay different processing rates and a currency conversion fee. Check Etsy's country table. — [Etsy Help: Payment Processing Fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy)
- Creativity Standards update of June 10, 2025: items made with computerized tools must be "produced based on a seller's original design", so templated designs no longer qualify as "Made by". "Sourced by a seller" listings must use real photos/video of the item or commercial images from the manufacturer. — [Value Added Resource, June 2025](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-creativity-standards-update-june-2025/); [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/recent-updates-to-etsy-standards-and-policies/); [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- Anecdotal Indian seller results: Diamondrensu (Surat, moissanite/lab-grown, on Etsy since 2018) reported 13,000+ sales and 5,000+ reviews. Ornamora (Surat) reported 1,800+ sales and a 4.9 rating by 2022. These figures come from Etsy shop pages and seller stories. — [DiamondrensuLabgrown on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/DiamondrensuLabgrown); [Ornamora on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/Ornamora)

**Amazon US**
- Fine Jewelry is a gated category. There is a $5,000 non-refundable application fee. Approval needs a Professional account, a quality review and documentation of material authenticity: grading reports for white diamonds of 0.75ct or more, precious metal and gemstone certificates with accurate karat marks, and wholesale invoices (within 180 days, 10+ units per product) per third-party guides. Used or vintage items are not allowed. — [the Centurion](https://news.centurionjewelry.com/jewelry-ecomm-tech/detail/how-to-sell-fine-jewelry-on-amazon); [BellaVix 2026](https://www.bellavix.com/amazon-jewelry-compliance-complete-guide-for-sellers-2026/)
- Referral fee: 20% on the first $250 of the item price and 5% on the portion above $250 (a $400 item pays $57.50). Referral fees are reported as frozen through 2026. Professional plan is $39.99/mo (standard Amazon fee; not re-verified here). — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-amazon-referral-fee-by-category); [Marginely](https://marginely.com/amazon-referral-fees-by-category/)

**eBay**
- Jewelry & Watches final value fee: 15% up to $5,000 and 9% above, plus $0.40 per order (over $10). — [eBay Seller Center](https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/selling/start-selling-on-ebay/seller-fees); [SellerFeeCalc](https://sellerfeecalc.com/ebay-fees)

**TikTok Shop US**
- Fine jewellery (any precious metal, diamonds, gems) is a restricted category that requires an application and approval. Invite-only approval can take up to 14 business days once an account manager submits the application. Each product type needs its own proof of authenticity (a single certificate cannot cover every item). Gem certificates from GIA/AGS/AIG/IGI are referenced. Plated or imitation pieces can be listed in standard categories. — [TikTok Shop Seller University: Jewelry Products Requirements](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4769331301730049); [Branvas 2026 guide](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/tiktok-shop-requirements-for-jewelry-sellers-2026-guide)

**Faire (wholesale)**
- North America-based brands pay 15% commission plus a $10 new-business fee on a buyer's first order. Brands outside North America pay an extra one-time 10% referral fee on a new retailer's first order (25% total), then 15% on reorders. Processing is 1.9-3.5% + $0.30. Faire Direct (retailers the brand invites) pays 0% commission. — [Faire Help Center: non-North America brands](https://www.faire.com/support/articles/360040446591); [Craftybase Faire calculator](https://craftybase.com/faire-fee-calculator)

### Inferences
- Effective take rates on a $400 order: Etsy ~10-11% without Offsite Ads and ~22-26% with them; Amazon ~14.4% referral alone; eBay ~15%; Shopify ~3-6% (Shopify Payments via a US entity) or ~5-8% (third-party gateway + 2% surcharge on Basic + FX). Etsy looks cheapest to start, but Offsite Ads becomes mandatory above $10k TTM.
- Wholesale via Faire generally needs a 2x wholesale-to-retail markup. With gold at around $4,250/oz, a brand's wholesale price may leave too little margin for retailers, so treat Faire as a secondary or opportunistic channel.
- The Amazon $5,000 gate plus certificate paperwork makes it a Year-2+ channel for a startup.

### Gaps
- No reliable Etsy jewellery category GMS share or lab-grown/moissanite category size was found. Etsy does not split this out publicly in accessible sources.
- TikTok Shop's current US referral commission for jewellery was not verified (fetch blocked). TikTok's US ownership/JV status in 2026 was not researched.
- Amazon's treatment of moissanite (fine vs fashion category) was not confirmed.

## 2. Marketing: ad costs, CAC, ROAS, AOV and repeat benchmarks

### Takeaway
Jewellery-specific paid benchmarks are thin and inconsistent. Cross-industry Meta medians (Aug 2025-Jul 2026) are about $39 CPA, $15 CPM and 1.9 ROAS. Jewellery commentary puts Meta ROAS at 1.8-2.5x and Google Shopping at about 2.5x. For a new brand selling a $300-600 AOV product, CAC of $80-200+ is a realistic planning range (inference). Repeat business matters a lot: Mejuri reports about 50% of revenue from repeat customers.

### Cited Findings
- Triple Whale (21,000+ brands, Aug 1, 2025-Jul 31, 2026), Meta: median CPA $38.99, CPM $15.06, ROAS 1.88, CVR 1.53%. Meta takes 66.9% of ad budgets. Google: median CPA $28.14, CPM $15.35, ROAS 3.27, 22.5% of spend. (snippet; jewellery row not visible) — [Triple Whale Facebook benchmarks](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks); [Triple Whale Google benchmarks](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/google-ads-benchmarks)
- CPM rose ~20% in 2025 across industries. For most DTC categories, ROAS of 1.5-2.5 is typical, and higher-ticket items like jewellery typically see 1.8-2.5x (secondary aggregators). — [Influee](https://influee.co/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks); [Mako Metrics](https://makometrics.com/blog/facebook-ads-roas-benchmark)
- Varos maintains a jewellery-specific Facebook ROAS benchmark page (April 2025), but its figures could not be retrieved. — [Varos](https://www.varos.com/benchmarks/facebook-roas-for-jewelry)
- Meta CPC for Apparel/Fashion & Jewelry was $0.71 vs $0.60 across all industries (LocalIQ/WordStream). — [LocalIQ](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/)
- Google Search: Apparel, Fashion & Jewelry average CPC is $4.31 and cost per lead $101. This vertical had one of the largest conversion-rate gains (+14.69% YoY) in WordStream's 2025 report. — [WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks); [PPC Chief](https://ppcchief.com/google-ads-benchmarks/apparel-fashion)
- Google Shopping: Jewelry & Accessories ROAS about 2.5x. Shopping CPC averages about $0.66 vs $5.26 on Search (all categories). — [FoundryCRO 2026](https://foundrycro.com/blog/google-shopping-benchmarks-by-category-2026/)
- One marketing vendor claims a $0.95 CPC / 4.5x ROAS as the best paid efficiency for jewellers in 2026. This is vendor content with unclear methodology, so treat it with caution. — [CUFinder](https://cufinder.io/blog/benchmarks/jewelers/)
- **Brilliant Earth (public, LGD-heavy)**: FY2025 net sales $437.5M (+3.6%), total orders 210,200 (+13%), repeat orders +15%. AOV was about $2,074 in Q2 2025 (vs $2,374) and $2,001 in Q4 2025 (-2.3%), with the decline attributed to mix shift toward fine jewellery. Q4 2025 had record $124.4M revenue but margin pressure and a loss. — [Brilliant Earth Q4 2025 release (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1866757/000162828026014992/brlt-4q25earningsrelease.htm); [Investing.com Q4 slides](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/brilliant-earth-q4-2025-slides-record-sales-offset-by-margin-pressure-93CH-4544541); [AlphaStreet](https://news.alphastreet.com/brlt-q4-2025-earnings-deep-dive/)
- **Mejuri**: estimated 2025 mejuri.com e-commerce revenue is $160M-178.5M (third-party estimates that conflict). AOV is about $225-250. About 50% of revenue comes from repeat customers, and ~25% of repeat buyers buy multiple items per year (secondary case study). — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/mejuri); [Grips Intelligence](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/mejuri.com); [FemFounded case study](https://femfounded.org/case-studies/mejuri/)

### Inferences
- Model math: at $400 AOV and 2.0x first-order ROAS, CAC is about $200. At 1.5x it is about $267. With a ~55-65% gross margin before marketing, first-order contribution is thin or negative. The model needs repeat purchases (Mejuri-like), organic/UGC and SEO to bring blended CAC below ~$100-150.
- Planning split: Meta prospecting (the main scalable channel), Google Shopping/Performance Max (high-intent, cheaper CPC), Pinterest (bridal/inspiration), and TikTok/Instagram organic UGC plus micro-influencer seeding. Seeding in LGD/moissanite relies on "looks like a $X,000 diamond" content.

### Gaps
- No verified jewellery-specific Meta CPA/CPM or Pinterest benchmarks for 2025-2026 were retrieved (Varos and Triple Whale jewellery rows blocked).
- No hard data was found on influencer seeding costs or conversion for jewellery.

## 3. Pricing, markups, landed cost and margins; return rates

### Takeaway
Lab-grown diamond wholesale prices have collapsed (select stones $80-105/ct wholesale by mid-2025), while retailers keep 80%+ markups over wholesale. Gold, not the stone, now dominates the cost of small LGD/moissanite pieces, since gold is about $4,250/oz. From August 2025 US tariffs on Indian jewellery swung between 50%, 18%, 10% (Section 122) and now reportedly a 12.5% Section 301 duty on top of the MFN rate. The $800 de minimis exemption is gone, which makes duty a material line item. Online jewellery return rates of 12-20% are typical.

### Cited Findings
**Stone and metal prices**
- LGD wholesale: select stones traded at $80-105 per carat by mid-2025, with wholesale down 37% YoY and a further 9% in Q2 2025. A 1ct lab diamond retails for about $1,000 or less, vs about $4,200 for a comparable natural stone. (snippet) — [JCK: Lab-Grown Wholesale Prices in Freefall](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/lab-grown-wholesale-freefall/); [Money.com](https://money.com/diamond-prices-plunge-lab-grown/)
- Paul Zimnisky's May 2026 update: retailers still capture a markup of more than 80% above wholesale on lab-grown, barely changed (1-2 percentage points) from 2025. — [Paul Zimnisky: Lab-Diamond Sales Grow as Prices Fall](https://www.paulzimnisky.com/Lab-Diamond-Sales-Grow-as-Prices-Fall); [JCK: narrative changing](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/lab-grown-narrative-is-changing/)
- Gold: $4,250.85/oz on Sep 24, 2026, down ~8.7% over the month but ~13% higher YoY. It was $4,418 on Sep 9, 2026. — [Fortune, Sep 21 2026](https://fortune.com/article/current-price-of-gold-09-21-2026/); [150currency](https://www.150currency.com/)

**US tariffs, duties and de minimis**
- From Aug 27, 2025, US tariffs on Indian gems and jewellery reached 50% (25% reciprocal + 25% Russia-oil penalty). Before that, cut and polished diamonds were duty-free and gold jewellery carried 5-7% MFN. The $800 de minimis exemption was suspended globally on Aug 29, 2025. — [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/news/u-s-imposes-50-tariff-on-indian-gems-and-jewelry-exports-plunge/); [RSM India note](https://www.rsm.global/india/sites/default/files/media/publications/2025/RSM%20India%20-%20US%20Tariff%20-%20High%20level%20note%20on%20Implication%20on%20G&J%20sector%20Exports%20April%202025.pdf)
- A US-India interim deal cut the IEEPA rate to 18% (reported variously as Nov 2025 or Feb 2026). On Feb 20, 2026 the Supreme Court invalidated the IEEPA tariffs. A 10% Section 122 global tariff applied from Feb 24, 2026, stacked on MFN duty. — [Global Trade Alert](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/from-ieepa-to-section-122); [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-Trump-Imposes-Section-122-Tariffs-After-Halting-IEEPA-Tariffs-Previews-New-Section-301-Investigations); [UN ESCAP](https://www.unescap.org/blog/india-us-bilateral-trade-agreement-and-implications-south-asia)
- Section 122 expired July 24, 2026. It was replaced the same day by Section 301 "forced labor" tariffs of 10% or 12.5% on about 60 countries, and India is reported in the 12.5% tier. The Court of International Trade struck down Section 122 in May 2026, with limited practical effect. (secondary; verify against USTR/CBP) — [UStariffcalc](https://ustariffcalc.com/section-122-expired-what-replaces-it.html); [Skadden, May 2026](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs); [Zonos tariff updates](https://zonos.com/us-tariff-updates)

**Returns**
- Online jewellery return rates: 12-15% (accessories and jewellery), 16.9-20% (jewellery e-commerce), and about 20% vs 17.5% for e-commerce overall. Best-in-class stores stay under 10% through imagery, sizing guides and material descriptions. Gifting and appearance mismatch are the main return drivers. — [Corso](https://www.corso.com/post-purchase-resource-center/ecommerce-return-rate-benchmarks-by-industry); [Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com/learn/ecommerce-return-rates); [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/ecommerce-return-rate-by-category)
- Brilliant Earth reported margin pressure in Q4 2025 despite record sales. The exact gross margin % could not be retrieved (SEC fetch blocked). — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/brilliant-earth-q4-2025-slides-record-sales-offset-by-margin-pressure-93CH-4544541)

### Inferences (illustrative landed-cost build; assumptions flagged)
Example: 14k yellow gold solitaire ring, 3.0 g finished weight, 1.0ct IGI-certified lab-grown round (or 1ct-equivalent moissanite).
- Gold: $4,250/oz ÷ 31.1035 = ~$136.6/g for 24k. 14k is 58.5% pure, so ~$80/g, and 3.0 g = **~$240**. Add ~5-10% casting loss / alloy margin, so ~$255 (assumption).
- Making/labour charges (India): **assumption of $25-50/piece.** No source was retrieved; get manufacturer quotes.
- 1ct LGD wholesale: $80-105/ct is for select parcels. Certified, well-cut 1ct D-G VS stones likely cost more (assume **$120-250**). IGI certificate: assume **$15-40** (not sourced). 1ct moissanite: assume **$30-80** (not sourced).
- Packaging, insert card, certificate folder: assume **$8-15**.
- Express courier India to US (DDP): assume **$25-45** per parcel (not sourced).
- US import duty: MFN on gold jewellery (5-7% per RSM/VisaVerge) + 12.5% Section 301 (if confirmed) = roughly **17.5-19.5% of customs value**. At a ~$450 customs value that is about **$80-90**. The rates changed four times in 12 months, so model 10%, 20% and 30% scenarios.
- Landed COGS (LGD version) is therefore about **$550-700**, and about **$450-550** for moissanite.
- Retail pricing: at a 2.0-2.5x landed markup, retail is $1,200-1,600, which is competitive with US LGD retail for a 1ct solitaire in 14k gold (~$1,000 for the stone alone at mainstream retail).
- Deductions from retail: payment fees ~3-4% (+2% Shopify third-party surcharge, +1% international card), returns reserve 12-20% of orders (on refunded orders, round-trip shipping and restocking are lost, not the goods), marketing CAC $150-300 for a high-AOV bridal piece.
- Rough contribution margin after CAC: gross margin ~50-60% → after payments/returns/shipping ~40-50% → after CAC ~10-30%. Plan to be contribution-positive only once repeat/organic share rises.
- Gold is roughly 40%+ of COGS, so gold-price hedging or dynamic pricing tied to the gold rate (as Indian jewellers do) is advisable. Lighter-weight designs and 10k options widen margin.

### Gaps
- No sourced India making-charge rates, IGI certificate fees, courier DDP rates or moissanite wholesale prices were retrieved. These are marked as assumptions above.
- The exact HTS rates (e.g., 7113.19.50) and the confirmed Section 301 rate applying to India as of Sept 2026 need CBP/USTR verification.
- No verified gross-margin figures for Brilliant Earth FY2025 or for LGD-focused D2C peers.

## 4. Trust builders and compliance

### Takeaway
US compliance rests on the FTC Jewelry Guides (clear "laboratory-grown" or equivalent qualified disclosure; karat disclosure) and the National Gold and Silver Stamping Act (a karat stamp must be accompanied by a registered trademark). The trust stack buyers expect includes IGI certificates (LGD standard), reviews, free resizing, 30-day returns, a warranty and a US return address. Marketplaces also require certificates.

### Cited Findings
- FTC Jewelry Guides: lab-grown diamonds must be clearly qualified, for example "laboratory-grown", "laboratory-created", "[manufacturer]-created" or other terms that clearly disclose man-made origin. Karat fineness must be disclosed. The 2018 revision removed the 10k minimum as long as the fineness is disclosed. — [FTC Jewelry Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/tools-consumers/jewelry-guides); [eCFR 16 CFR Part 23](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-23); [National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/4826-8-key-changes-the-ftc-made-to-the-jewelry-guides-a-refresher); [FTC "In the Loupe"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/loupe-advertising-diamond-gemstones-pearls)
- National Gold and Silver Stamping Act: an item stamped with a quality/fineness mark must also carry the maker's registered trademark. — [the Centurion summary](https://news.centurionjewelry.com/articles/detail/a-plain-language-guide-to-changes-and-important-updates-in-ftc-revised-jewe)
- Amazon requires grading reports for white diamonds of 0.75ct+ and precious-metal certificates. TikTok Shop requires per-product authenticity proof and cites GIA/AGS/IGI. — [BellaVix](https://www.bellavix.com/how-to-get-approved-to-sell-jewelry-on-amazon-requirements-and-quality-review/); [TikTok Seller University](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4769331301730049)
- Successful Indian Etsy LGD/moissanite shops advertise IGI/GIA certification, customization and payment plans. — [DiamondrensuLabgrown](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/DiamondrensuLabgrown)

### Inferences
- Minimum trust set for launch: IGI certificate on every stone of 0.5ct+ (with a laser inscription match), a "14K" stamp plus a registered-trademark stamp (register the US trademark early), a 30-day free-return policy with a US returns address (3PL or returns consolidator), free first resizing, a 1-year or lifetime manufacturing warranty, and photo/video reviews imported from Etsy.
- Returns shipped back to India are slow and costly, and duty drawback is complex. A US returns address is both a trust signal and a cost control.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data was found on how each trust signal lifts conversion for jewellery.
- The US trademark registration timeline and cost for stamping were not researched.

## 5. Case studies: Indian-founded and small brands selling to the US online

### Takeaway
The proven Indian path is Surat/Jaipur manufacturers moving onto Etsy (2017-2018 cohort) and scaling through reviews, customization and moissanite/LGD value pricing. Diamondrensu reached 13k+ sales. Scaled D2C brands (Brilliant Earth, Mejuri) show that repeat purchases and moving into fine jewellery at lower price points drive order growth, even while AOV falls.

### Cited Findings
- Diamondrensu (Surat) moved from local natural-diamond trade to Etsy moissanite/LGD in 2018 and reached 13,000+ sales and 5,000+ reviews (seller-provided story; anecdotal). — [Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/DiamondrensuLabgrown)
- Ornamora (Surat; family diamond business) opened its Etsy store in 2018 and had 1,800+ sales and 650+ reviews (4.9 rating) by 2022 (anecdotal). — [Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/Ornamora)
- Other Indian-origin US-facing LGD brands found: DiAi Designs (Indian brand, US-targeted LGD site) and Diamond Atelier (NY-based Etsy LGD seller since 2019). No revenue data was available. — [DiAi Designs](https://diaidesigns.com/); [DiamondAtelierInc](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/shop/DiamondAtelierInc)
- Brilliant Earth grew orders 13% and repeat orders 15% in FY2025 while AOV fell to about $2,000 due to the fine-jewellery mix shift. It has also faced margin pressure, a warning sign for LGD price deflation. — [Brilliant Earth IR](https://investors.brilliantearth.com/news-releases/news-release-details/brilliant-earth-reports-first-quarter-2025-results); [AlphaStreet](https://news.alphastreet.com/brlt-q4-2025-earnings-deep-dive/)
- The 50% tariff period (Aug 2025-Feb 2026) was projected to cut Indian G&J exports to the US by ~15% ($1.8-2B). The US accounts for 30-33% of India's G&J exports ($10-12B/yr). — [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/news/u-s-imposes-50-tariff-on-indian-gems-and-jewelry-exports-plunge/); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/gold-jewellery-sector-raise-alarms-over-50-us-tariffs)

### Inferences
- Lessons: (1) start on Etsy to build reviews and cash flow; (2) differentiate with original designs (Etsy's 2025 rules penalize templated or sourced goods); (3) move buyers to an owned Shopify store and email list for repeat purchases; (4) keep flexibility against tariff shocks (a US entity, a DDP pricing buffer, or US-side final assembly or stock); (5) LGD price deflation erodes stone-value positioning, so sell on design and gold rather than "carat for the money".
- Failure risks: margin squeeze from tariffs, LGD commoditization (Etsy is flooded with Indian moissanite sellers, so price competition is intense), Offsite Ads fees, and returns and chargebacks on high-ticket items without a US returns base.

### Gaps
- No well-documented Reddit or press post-mortems of failed Indian D2C jewellery brands in the US were found in the time available.
- No revenue-verified Indian-founded Shopify LGD brand case study in the US was found.
