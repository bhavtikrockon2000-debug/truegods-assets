# Caratbee (CaratBee, caratbee.com): Products, Pricing, Channels, Marketing

**Method note (observed 2026-09-25):** caratbee.com, etsy.com, trustpilot.com and natlawreview.com were all **blocked by this environment's network egress proxy**. Direct fetches and curl returned 403 or EGRESS_BLOCKED, so nothing on those sites could be opened directly. All facts below come from **search-engine snippets and summaries** of those pages (URLs cited), plus a Meta Ad Library API query. Prices come from search snippets, so their capture date is unknown. They were seen on 2026-09-25 but may be cached older. Anything a snippet reported is marked "per snippet".

## 1. Company identity and positioning

### Takeaway
CaratBee is a US-registered brand (Dover, Delaware address, "CaratBee LLC") run from Surat, India, by CEO Lav Bhimani. It sells lab-grown diamond and moissanite fine jewelry direct-to-consumer to the US. Its main pitch is "direct from our own manufacturing facility", IGI-certified, ethical/eco and up to 70% cheaper than mined diamonds.

### Cited Findings
- A July 31, 2024 press release datelined **Surat, India** announced "premium lab grown diamond ring collections" and quoted **CEO Lav Bhimani** — [Yahoo Finance/ACCESSWIRE](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/introducing-beauty-innovation-caratbees-premium-140000332.html); [Accesswire](https://www.accesswire.com/894773/introducing-the-beauty-of-innovation-with-caratbees-premium-lab-grown-diamond-ring-collections)
- A June 19, 2026 press release datelined **Dover, Delaware** says the "direct-to-manufacturer model" delivers IGI-certified lab-grown diamond and moissanite rings more cheaply. It also says CaratBee "sells directly from its own manufacturing facility to customers without the traditional chain of wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and showroom markups" — [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality); syndicated on [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without) and [WBOC](https://www.wboc.com/online_features/press_releases/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality/article_0ba5d01d-f891-59be-b54a-86b670140530.html)
- An Aug 29, 2024 press release datelined Dover, Delaware covered the carbon footprint of lab-grown vs mined diamonds, an eco/sustainability angle — [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/739154043/study-finds-lab-grown-diamonds-have-a-much-lower-carbon-footprint-than-mined-diamonds-let-s-explore-with-caratbee)
- The brand claims "15+ years of industry expertise". It says it specializes in "production and distribution of lab-grown diamonds, moissanite" and jewelry. It has "an office located in Delaware but operates primarily online" (per snippet of site) — [caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/); [About](https://caratbee.com/pages/about-us)
- The Etsy shop bio says the owner "Lav" has "over 10 years of experience in Diamond Jewelry". Note this does not match the site's "15+ years" claim — [Etsy CaratBeeLabGrown](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLabGrown)
- Other claims:
  - "Trusted by 1,000+ happy customers worldwide, 125+ verified reviews on Trustpilot"
  - "ethically sourced", "conflict-free… Kimberley Process"
  - "save 25% compared to other brands"
  - collections "up to 70% more affordable than mined diamond engagement rings"
  - Sources: [caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/); [Yahoo/ACCESSWIRE](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/introducing-beauty-innovation-caratbees-premium-140000332.html)

### Inferences
- The model is an Indian (Surat) manufacturer/exporter with a US LLC front end, the same set-up a new India-based competitor would use. Its differentiators are price transparency and "manufacturer-direct", not brand prestige.
- Press releases on paid wires (Accesswire, EIN Presswire) are probably used for SEO backlinks and "as seen on" credibility, not for real media coverage.

### Gaps
- Founding year, headcount, revenue and the factory's size or location inside Surat could not be confirmed.

## 2. Product catalogue

### Takeaway
The catalogue is bridal-led: engagement rings (lab diamond and moissanite), wedding bands and bridal sets. There are also stud earrings, tennis bracelets, pendants/necklaces and loose diamonds. Settings come in 10K/14K/18K white, yellow and rose gold. Collections are organised heavily by carat, shape and stone, which reads as an SEO-driven structure. Custom design is a core offer.

## 3. Pricing and promotions

### Takeaway
Prices are shown as a crossed-out "regular price" with a near-permanent **30% off** sale price, ending in .99 (e.g. $1,511.41 → $1,057.99). Moissanite 1ct engagement rings run about $500–800. 1ct lab-diamond rings run about $1,000–3,000. Bridal sets start around $327. There is also a 2% discount for wire transfers over $1,000.

  - 3.5ct moissanite halo: about $1,058
  - lab-diamond hero rings: about $1,500–1,600
- No price for a comparable 1ct lab solitaire in 14K, a lab tennis bracelet or lab studs was surfaced in snippets.

### Takeaway
The main channel is a **Shopify** DTC store (caratbee.com) with a Shop app listing. There are **two Etsy shops**: CaratBeeLabGrown (Surat, India) and CaratBeeLLC (listed as US). No evidence was found of Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, Faire or wholesale.

### Cited Findings
- **Shopify:**
  - the URL structure is Shopify-standard (/collections/, /products/, /pages/, /blogs/, /policies/refund-policy)
  - the brand has a Shopify **Shop app** merchant page ("CaratBee - Shop", shop.app/m/rwb702t6yh)
  - Sources: [shop.app](https://shop.app/m/rwb702t6yh); [refund policy URL](https://caratbee.com/policies/refund-policy)
- **Etsy "CaratBeeLabGrown":** located in **Surat, India**, tagline "IGI Certified Lab Diamond Engagement Rings". Owner "Lav" — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLabGrown)
- **Etsy "CaratBeeLLC":** a search summary lists it as located in the United States. It sells rings, bands, necklaces and bracelets and ran an "up to 35% off" banner. Reviews quoted praise responsiveness, customisation help and certification — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)

## 6. Policies (shipping, returns, resizing, warranty, certificates, financing)

### Takeaway
CaratBee offers free worldwide shipping in about 15–22 days (express $75). The return window is 30 days in the refund policy but 10 days in other copy. Resizing is reimbursed up to $100 at a local jeweller. A short 10-day workmanship warranty applies. Digital IGI certificates are free, with hard copies extra. Payment options include Klarna, Afterpay and Splitit, but not Affirm.

### Cited Findings
- **Shipping:** free worldwide shipping delivered in 15–22 days, or express for $75 in 14–18 days (per snippet) — [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- **Returns:**
  - the refund policy gives a full refund within **30 days** of receipt, in original condition with box and certificate (GIA/IGI/GRA), refunded within 7 business days of receipt — [Refund policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/refund-policy)
  - this conflicts with a site summary citing a "**10-day return policy**" — [caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/)
- **Resizing:** one-time free resizing reimbursed **up to $100** at the customer's local jeweller, within 30 days — [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- **Warranty:** within **10 days** of purchase they fix loose prongs, polish or rhodium-plate — [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- **Certificates:** free IGI digital certificate for lab-grown diamonds, with the physical copy arranged on request and sold as a product — [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs); [Hard copy product](https://caratbee.com/products/certificate-hardcopy)
- **Payments/financing:** Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover, Stripe, PayPal, **AfterPay, Klarna, Splitit**, plus wire transfer (2% off over $1,000). Affirm was not listed. Support is via chat and care@caratbee.com — [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)

### Inferences
- The long shipping times (15–22 days even for "express" 14–18 days) are consistent with made-to-order production in India. The "express" option barely differs from standard.
- Outsourced resizing and a 10-day warranty are weak compared with US DTC brands, which usually offer lifetime warranties and free in-house resizing. A competitor could beat these terms.

### Gaps
- Multi-currency support was not confirmed.
- Duties/tariff handling for US imports was not found.
- The exact current return window is unclear (10 vs 30 days); the live site should be checked.
