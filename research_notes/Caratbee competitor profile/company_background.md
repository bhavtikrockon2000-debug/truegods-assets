# Caratbee (caratbee.com): Company Background, Structure and Operations

Research date: 2026-09-25. **Method limits:** The egress proxy blocked direct page fetches from caratbee.com, YourStory, OpenCorporates, Justia Trademarks, ProvenExpert, NatLawReview, AccessNewswire and WBOC. All findings below come from search-engine result snippets and summaries of those pages, not from full-page reads. Treat each detail as "reported by the cited page, per search snippet". Nothing here was checked against a primary registry: no MCA, Delaware SOS or USPTO record was read directly.

**Disambiguation:** The only "CaratBee" jewellery business that turned up is the Surat-based lab-grown diamond and moissanite DTC brand at caratbee.com. Do not confuse it with **CaratLane** (Titan-owned, Mumbai), which showed up in several searches. No other "Carat Bee" jewellery brand was found.

## 4. Manufacturing and sourcing

### Takeaway
CaratBee claims its own manufacturing ("sells directly from its own manufacturing facility"). It sells IGI-certified lab-grown diamonds (both CVD and HPHT are discussed) and moissanite with GRA reports, in sterling silver and gold. No SEZ, IEC or GJEPC details were found.

### Cited Findings
- "Sells directly from its own manufacturing facility to customers without the traditional chain of wholesalers, distributors, retailers" — [EIN Presswire 2026](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality)
- IGI certification is offered, and a physical IGI hard-copy certificate is sold as a separate product. That implies the default certificate is digital — [caratbee.com certificate product](https://caratbee.com/products/certificate-hardcopy)
- The site explains CVD vs HPHT and notes that IGI reports state the growth method — [caratbee blog](https://caratbee.com/blogs/lab-grown-diamonds/hpht-vs-cvd-lab-grown-diamonds-key-differences); [certified vs uncertified](https://caratbee.com/blogs/education/certified-vs-uncertified-diamonds-which-one-should-you-choose)
- Moissanite comes with a GRA certificate — [caratbee FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- Metals: sterling silver and gold. Listings include 14k rose gold (e.g., "Alia 4 Carat Round Solitaire… 14k Rose Gold") — [EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market); [product page](https://caratbee.com/products/alia-4-carat-round-solitaire-lab-grown-engagement-ring-in-14k-rose-gold)
- Loose lab-grown diamonds are also sold — [caratbee loose LGD](https://caratbee.com/collections/loose-lab-grown-diamonds)
- Claims Kimberley Process adherence and "conflict-free" sourcing (odd for lab-grown stones; marketing copy) — [Etsy CaratBeeLLC](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)

### Inferences
- The registered/contact address is a small office in a commercial tower, so casting and setting are likely done in a separate unit or by Surat contract manufacturers. The "own facility" claim cannot be verified.
- The stones are likely bought from Surat LGD growers or polishers (Surat dominates CVD growing) rather than grown in-house. There is no evidence of reactors.

### Gaps
- No evidence on IEC, GJEPC membership, SEZ/SEEPZ unit, BIS hallmarking or gold karat range (10k/18k/platinum unconfirmed).
- No evidence of GIA grading.

## 5. US operating model (fulfilment, address, duties, shipping)

### Takeaway
The evidence points to shipping from India to US customers, with duties and taxes charged to the customer (DAP, not DDP), 7–10 business-day processing, and a paid express option. The US presence appears to be a Delaware LLC/registered address, not a warehouse.

### Cited Findings
- Standard processing: dispatch within 7–10 business days; no shipping on weekends or holidays — [caratbee shipping policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/shipping-policy)
- Express shipping add-on at $75: dispatch within 2–5 business days — [caratbee express shipping](https://caratbee.com/products/express-shipping)
- "CaratBee is not responsible for any customs and taxes… such fees are the responsibility of the customer". If the buyer refuses duties and customs does not release the parcel, no refund is given — [caratbee shipping policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/shipping-policy); [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- All shipments are "100% insured", with signature on delivery. Hold-for-pickup at FedEx/DHL locations is possible — [caratbee FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)
- Free shipping and 30-day returns on engagement rings; complimentary resizing — [caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/); [EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market)
- Contact emails support@caratbee.com and care@caratbee.com. Listed phone/WhatsApp "+1 3332912750" — [caratbee contact](https://caratbee.com/pages/contact-us) (snippet). **Note:** 333 is not an assigned US area code, so the number may have been transcribed wrongly in the snippet, or it is a non-US/virtual number.
- A Trustpilot reviewer reports CaratBee paid their import duty and expedited shipping after a delay (goodwill gesture) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com)
- Markets served: US, Canada, UK, Germany, Australia — [EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market)

### Inferences
- The FedEx/DHL references, the customs-duty clauses and the Surat Etsy location together imply direct courier export from India (likely an express/courier export, possibly under ecommerce export rules). There is no US stock.
- With US tariffs on Indian goods elevated in 2025–26, the customer-pays-duties (DAP) policy is a real friction point for US buyers. A DDP competitor can exploit this, but CaratBee's current tariff handling on US orders was not confirmed.
- Total US delivery time is likely about 2–3 weeks standard (7–10 business days production plus international transit and customs). This is inferred, not stated.

### Gaps
- No confirmed US warehouse or US returns address.
- No explicit DDP/DAP statement for US orders specifically, and no tariff-surcharge policy was found.
