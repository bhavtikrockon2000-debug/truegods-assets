# Caratbee Proves the Model, Leaves Gaps Open

Caratbee (caratbee.com) is a small, bootstrapped business in Surat that runs the same playbook a new India-based gold-and-lab-stone brand would run. It pairs a Surat diamond-trade team with a Delaware "CaratBee LLC" front end, a Shopify store and two Etsy shops. It sells IGI-certified lab-grown diamond and moissanite jewellery in 10K, 14K and 18K gold, shipped by courier from India. It launched around **2020**. It has no visible outside funding, no earned press, about **5,100 Instagram followers**, no detectable Meta ads, but an active **Google Ads** account (Search, Shopping, Performance Max and video) run by a verified US advertiser, **"UltraCarat Inc"**, and roughly **37 to 200+ Trustpilot reviews** (the count grew over 2024 to 2026) at a **4.5 to 4.8** TrustScore. Its catalogue is led by engagement rings: 1ct moissanite solitaires from about **$535**, 1ct lab-diamond rings at **$1,000 to $3,000**, and big-look moissanite and tennis bracelets. Almost everything is shown at a permanent "30% off". Customers praise the sparkle, the value and the responsive staff. The damaging complaints are about **made-to-order delays that miss proposal dates**. One reviewer also says the company offered a gift to change a negative review. For a new entrant, Caratbee shows that the model works at small scale, and it also shows where to beat it. Its site policies make US buyers **pay import duty on delivery** (though its latest Google ads promise "no hidden fees" and free US delivery, which a test order should check), now about **15.5-16%** on Indian jewellery with no de minimis exemption. Its warranty lasts **10 days**, it caps returns, it outsources resizing, it buys demand on Google rather than creating it on social, and it sells almost nothing in the everyday gold categories (chains, pendants, studs) where US lab-grown growth is moving. A brand that ships duty-paid, stocks fast-moving items in the US, and leads with everyday design competes where Caratbee is weakest.

**A note on evidence.** Every Caratbee fact below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages. The research environment's proxy blocked direct fetches of caratbee.com, Etsy, Trustpilot and several registries. Prices and review counts are therefore snapshots with unknown capture dates. Where site copy contradicts itself, both versions are reported.

## A Surat diamond family behind a Delaware LLC

Caratbee presents itself in two ways, depending on where you look. The Indian side is an office at **701 Pavitraa Point, Yogi Chowk, Surat**, with an Indian phone number and a Gmail address ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/companies/caratbee); [ProvenExpert](https://www.provenexpert.com/en-us/caratbee/)). Pavitraa Point is a G+7 commercial office tower, not an industrial unit ([Pavitraa Realty](https://pavitraarealty.com/pavitraa-point/)). The US side is "CaratBee LLC", described on Etsy as having "an office located in Delaware but operates primarily online" ([Etsy CaratBeeLLC](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)). Its 2024 and 2026 press releases carry a **Dover, Delaware** dateline ([EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality)). Dover is the default address for many registered-agent services, so the LLC most likely handles payments and marketplace onboarding, not operations. That is an inference; no Delaware or Indian MCA record was retrieved. The footer uses "CaratBee™" rather than "®" ([caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/)), and no USPTO filing surfaced. A ZaubaCorp search on the CEO's surname found **Bhimani Diamonds LLP** in Surat, but nothing ties it to Caratbee ([ZaubaCorp](https://www.zaubacorp.com/BHIMANI-DIAMONDS-LLP-ABA-2755)).

Sources disagree on who leads the company. YourStory and Crunchbase profiles name **Mohit Boda** as founder, with "over 15 years of expertise in the diamond industry, launching as an e-commerce brand in 2020" ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/companies/caratbee); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/caratbee)). Press releases from July 2024 and June 2026 quote **Lav Bhimani as CEO** ([Yahoo Finance/AccessWire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/introducing-beauty-innovation-caratbees-premium-140000332.html); [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality)). The Etsy shop owner is also "Lav" ([Etsy CaratBeeLabGrown](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLabGrown)). They could be co-founders, or a founder and a later CEO; the sources do not settle it, and neither man has a findable LinkedIn profile or interview. Experience claims also conflict. The site says the team spent 15 years cutting and polishing diamonds before moving into jewellery around 2019 ([caratbee.com About](https://caratbee.com/pages/about-us)), while Lav's Etsy bio says "over 10 years" ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLabGrown)). Both fit a familiar Surat pattern, in which a polishing family moves downstream into direct-to-consumer jewellery.

There is no evidence of outside funding, revenue disclosures or earned press in outlets such as ET, Inc42, JCK or Rapaport. The public footprint consists of **paid wire releases**: AccessWire in July 2024, EIN Presswire in August 2024, August 2025 and June 2026. These were syndicated to Yahoo Finance, NatLawReview and local TV-station sites ([AccessNewswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/consumer-and-retail-products/introducing-the-beauty-of-innovation-with-caratbees-premium-lab-grown-d-894773); [EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market); [WBOC](https://www.wboc.com/online_features/press_releases/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality/article_0ba5d01d-f891-59be-b54a-86b670140530.html)). The brand's own claims rose from "1,000+ happy customers" and "125+ Trustpilot reviews" to "over 500 verified five-star reviews" by August 2025 and "175+ unique designs" by 2026 ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/companies/caratbee); [EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market); [EIN 2026](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality)). The business is real and growing, but it is small, probably a few thousand orders over its lifetime. That size estimate is an inference, not a sourced figure.

The catalogue centres on engagement rings in lab-grown diamond and moissanite, plus wedding bands, bridal sets, stud earrings, tennis bracelets, pendants and necklaces, and loose lab diamonds ([caratbee.com collections](https://caratbee.com/collections)). Collections are built around search terms: "1 carat moissanite ring", "2 carat moissanite ring", "4 carat moissanite rings", "emerald cut moissanite ring" ([1ct moissanite](https://caratbee.com/collections/1-carat-moissanite-ring); [4ct moissanite](https://caratbee.com/collections/4-carat-moissanite-rings)). Products carry a woman's first name followed by keyword-heavy descriptions, such as "Carla 5.5 Carat Oval… Moissanite… in **10k Yellow Gold**" or "Callie 1 Carat Round… Lab Grown… in **14k Rose Gold**". Metals run across **10K, 14K and 18K** in white, yellow and rose ([Carla](https://caratbee.com/products/carla-5-5-carat-oval-cut-solitaire-pave-moissanite-diamond-ring-in-10k-yellow-gold); [Callie](https://caratbee.com/products/callie-1-carat-round-solitaire-pave-lab-grown-engagement-ring-in-14k-rose-gold)). The range leans toward a "big look for less" customer, with 3.5-5.5ct moissanite solitaires and **24-34ct lab-grown tennis bracelets** ([Dalia](https://caratbee.com/products/dalia-34-carat-oval-cut-lab-grown-diamonds-tennis-bracelet); [Navy](https://caratbee.com/products/navy-heart-shaped-lab-grown-diamonds-tennis-bracelet)). The pricing mechanics tell you more than the price points. The odd "regular" prices, such as $1,511.41, are exactly the sale price divided by 0.70. That means the list price is worked backwards from the price the brand actually charges, and the **30% discount is effectively the everyday price**. Seasonal promotions of 10-25% sit on top, especially around Black Friday and Valentine's Day, and Etsy banners advertise "up to 35% off" ([1ct moissanite blog](https://caratbee.com/blogs/moissanite-diamonds/how-much-does-a-1-carat-moissanite-ring-cost); [Etsy CaratBeeLLC](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)). Wire transfers above $1,000 get a further 2% ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). The value pitch rests on "direct from our own manufacturing facility… without the traditional chain of wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and showroom markups", "up to 70% more affordable than mined", and "save 25% compared to other brands" ([EIN 2026](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/920688032/caratbee-explains-why-certified-lab-grown-diamond-jewelry-costs-less-without-compromising-quality); [Yahoo/AccessWire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/introducing-beauty-innovation-caratbees-premium-140000332.html)). The "own facility" claim cannot be verified, since the registered address is an office tower. Lab stones come with a free digital IGI report, and a **hard-copy IGI certificate is sold as a separate paid product**. Moissanite comes with a GRA card ([certificate product](https://caratbee.com/products/certificate-hardcopy); [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). The site also claims "conflict-free… Kimberley Process" sourcing. The Kimberley Process is a certification scheme for mined diamonds, so the claim does not apply to lab-grown stones and muddles the message ([Etsy CaratBeeLLC](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)).

These prices fall within the landed-cost estimate for an India-made 14K, 1ct IGI lab solitaire: **$515-740** including 15.5% duty. At a 2.0-2.5x markup, that implies a $1,100-1,800 retail price ([Fortune gold price](https://fortune.com/article/current-price-of-gold-09-21-2026/); [Edahn Golan](https://www.edahngolan.com/lab-grown-diamond-wholesale-prices-2025/)). Caratbee's lab hero rings sit at the lower end of that band. It can price there partly because the customer, not Caratbee, pays the duty.

## Google Ads, SEO and Etsy, with almost no paid social

The store runs on **Shopify** (standard /collections/, /products/ and /policies/ URLs, plus a Shop app merchant page) ([shop.app](https://shop.app/m/rwb702t6yh)). Caratbee also runs **two Etsy storefronts**. "CaratBeeLabGrown" lists Surat, India as its location. "CaratBeeLLC" presents as a US LLC ([Etsy CaratBeeLabGrown](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLabGrown); [Etsy CaratBeeLLC](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CaratBeeLLC)). This looks like a test of whether a US-seller identity converts better, and possibly a hedge against losing one Etsy account (both inferences). A **Walmart Marketplace** seller called "CaratBee" also exists ([Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/seller/102550109/cp/shopall)). Researchers found no Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop or Faire presence, but that absence is not confirmed. The brand says it serves the US, Canada, UK, Germany and Australia ([EIN 2025](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/836463228/caratbee-introduces-affordable-silver-and-gold-jewelry-collection-for-global-market)).

Customer acquisition is built around **search intent**. Caratbee runs a substantial **Google Ads** account (analysed in the next section from Ads Transparency Center screenshots; spend is not disclosed there). Paid search captures shoppers already typing queries like "moissanite engagement ring" or "1 carat lab diamond ring", and Google Search clicks for jewellery average about **$4.31** (see the market report). The organic side supports the same intent. The blog targets price-intent searches with posts such as "How Much Does a 1 Carat Lab Grown Diamond Cost?", "Moissanite or Lab Diamond? Detailed Cost Breakdown" and "Moissanite Engagement Rings… Under $700" ([blog](https://caratbee.com/blogs/lab-grown-diamonds/how-much-does-a-1-carat-lab-grown-diamond-cost); [cost breakdown](https://caratbee.com/blogs/education/moissanite-or-lab-diamond-detailed-cost-breakdown-value-guide); [under $700](https://caratbee.com/blogs/engagement-rings/choose-the-unique-moissanite-engagement-rings-to-buy-under-700)). Separate WordPress and Wix blogs appear to exist to build links back to the main site ([WordPress](https://caratbee.wordpress.com/2024/03/07/round-engagement-rings-your-pathway-to-everlasting-love/); [Wix](https://caratbeesocial.wixsite.com/pages/post/discover-the-charm-of-elongated-cushion-engagement-rings-with-caratbee)). Social reach is small: Instagram @caratbee has about **5,119 followers across 80 posts** ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/caratbee/)). A Meta Ad Library query on 25 September 2026 returned **zero ads for "caratbee.com"** across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and India. The three active "CaratBee" keyword hits came from unrelated advertisers ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/)). TikTok has "Caratbee ring reviews" discovery pages, which points to some user-generated content, but no influencer programme is visible ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/caratbee-ring-reviews)). Payments cover cards, PayPal, Stripe, **Afterpay, Klarna and Splitit**, but not Affirm ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). Klarna on Shopify requires a local entity, and Shop Pay Installments is limited to US, Canadian and UK merchants ([Klarna](https://www.klarna.com/international/enterprise/platforms-and-partners/shopify/); [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/buy-now-pay-later)). So Caratbee's BNPL offering is probably the clearest concrete benefit of its Delaware LLC.

## A verified US advertiser, "UltraCarat Inc", buying engagement-ring searches

Screenshots of the Google Ads Transparency Center taken by the user on 25 September 2026 (saved in `research_notes/Caratbee competitor profile/google_ads_screenshots/`) show dozens of live CaratBee ads. **Every one is run by a verified advertiser named "UltraCarat Inc"**, not "CaratBee LLC". "Inc" means a US corporation (the state is not visible), so Caratbee's US set-up is likely more than the Delaware LLC seen on Etsy. That is the entity Google verified, and it is the most likely owner of the Shopify, payments and BNPL accounts. No state registry record was checked.

**Formats.** The account runs the full Google stack: Shopping and Performance Max product cards with red "-29%" and "-30%" badges, responsive search ads with sitelinks ("1 / 2 / 3 / 4 Carat Lab Grown Rings"), display banners and short video ads. The search ads carry a Google seller rating of **5.0 from 46 ratings**, a "Most items 30+ days" return annotation, a "Up to US$100 off, limited time" promotion extension and a **WhatsApp** message extension.

**What is advertised.** It is all engagement rings: radiant, emerald, pear, marquise, oval, cushion and east-west solitaires, halos and five-stone rings, sold under first names (Casey, Aria, Darcy, Cindy) at 1 to 4 carats. No chains, pendants, studs or everyday gold appear, which confirms the category gap described below.

**Price anchors in the ads:** radiant lab rings **$697**, east-west rings **from $697**, lab-grown emerald rings **from $827.99**, a 4ct marquise **from $897**, and radiant moissanite rings **under $999**.

**Messages, in order of repetition:**
- Savings against traditional jewellers: "70% off", "Save up to 70% vs traditional jewelers", "70% lower price vs retail", "Factory to finger prices – no retail markup".
- Certification: "IGI Certified Lab Diamonds", "Conflict free".
- Customisation: "Made for you – fully customized diamond rings at no extra cost", "Design your own ring".
- Risk reversal: "30 days free returns, no questions asked", "Free ring resizing guarantee", "30 days return & resizing".
- Delivery and price transparency: "Free Express Shipping – Fast Delivery", "Certified, custom & **delivered free in the USA**", "Transparent pricing, **no hidden fees, what you see is what you pay**".

**Where the ads and the site disagree.** Several ad promises contradict the policies the research found on caratbee.com: that customers pay US duties and taxes on delivery, that free shipping takes 15-22 days, and that resizing is reimbursed up to $100 at a local jeweller. Either Caratbee has recently moved to duty-paid, faster shipping and in-house resizing and not yet updated its FAQ, or the ads over-promise. Under the FTC Act's ban on deceptive advertising and Google's misrepresentation policy, "no hidden fees" next to a buyer-pays-duty policy would be a compliance risk. The "transparent pricing" claim also sits beside permanent -29% and -30% badges calculated from inflated reference prices. **A test order is the only way to settle this**, and it matters, because duty-paid delivery is the main gap this report recommends exploiting.

**What this changes.** Caratbee is not an organic-only brand. It pays for high-intent search traffic across the whole ring catalogue, with a mature account (seller ratings, promotions, messaging, video). A new entrant will bid against it on terms such as "radiant lab diamond ring" and "moissanite engagement ring". Staying out of bridal search at the start, and leading with everyday gold where Caratbee does not advertise, avoids a bidding war with an established account.

## Made to order in India, with the duty bill left to the buyer

Caratbee ships every order from India by insured courier (FedEx or DHL, signature on delivery, with hold-at-location available). There is no evidence of US stock, a US warehouse or a US returns address ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). Its published delivery times contradict each other. The FAQ snippet says **free worldwide shipping arrives in 15-22 days, and $75 express in 14-18 days** ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). The shipping policy and express product page say **standard orders dispatch in 7-10 business days, and the $75 express option dispatches in 2-5 business days** ([shipping policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/shipping-policy); [express shipping](https://caratbee.com/products/express-shipping)). The two versions may measure different things (delivery versus dispatch), or one may be out of date. Either way, a standard buyer waits roughly two to three weeks, and "express" saves less than a week at best.

The biggest structural weakness is **duties**. Caratbee "is not responsible for any customs and taxes… such fees are the responsibility of the customer." If a buyer refuses to pay and customs holds the parcel, **no refund is given** ([shipping policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/shipping-policy); [FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). In shipping terms this is DAP (delivered at place, duties unpaid). The policy became much more costly for buyers in 2025-26. The US suspended the **$800 de minimis exemption on 29 August 2025**. CBP then made the suspension **indefinite** through interim final rules effective **24 June 2026 for courier and freight** and **24 July 2026 for mail** ([FR 2026-12670](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other); [FR 2026-12669](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry)). A reviews summary cited a "June 24, 2026 CBP rule" without a source; that date matches the courier rule. Every Caratbee parcel now needs a customs entry. It pays **5.5% MFN duty under HTSUS 7113.19 plus the 10% Section 301 tariff in force since 24 July 2026**, about **15.5-16%** in total ([USITC HTS](https://hts.usitc.gov/search?query=711319); [Federal Register 2026-15181](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/28/2026-15181/notice-of-actions-in-section-301-investigations-of-acts-policies-and-practices-of-various-economies); [GJEPC](https://gjepc.org/solitaire/us-retains-10-tariff-on-indian-exports-under-new-section-301-regime/)). Courier brokerage fees come on top. On a $1,058 moissanite ring, the buyer can face a door bill of roughly $150-170 plus fees, which they did not see at checkout. That figure is an inference from the published rates; researchers found no evidence that Caratbee has moved to duty-paid delivery or added a tariff line at checkout. Duties have already come up in complaints: in one Trustpilot dispute, Caratbee **paid the buyer's import duty** as a goodwill gesture ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com)).

After-sale terms are thin by US standards, and the site's own copy contradicts itself. The refund policy promises a **full refund within 30 days** of receipt, in original condition with box and certificate, paid within 7 business days ([refund policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/refund-policy)). Another summary of the site cites a **10-day return policy** ([caratbee.com](https://caratbee.com/)). Both versions are live-site evidence and should be checked directly. The FAQ adds a **cap of two returns or exchanges per customer per year**, and the refund policy reportedly **excludes wrong-size orders** ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs); [refund policy](https://caratbee.com/policies/refund-policy)). Resizing is one free job reimbursed **up to $100 at the customer's local jeweller** within 30 days. The workmanship warranty (loose prongs, polishing, rhodium) lasts only **10 days** ([FAQ](https://caratbee.com/pages/faqs)). Taken together, this is the operating model of a Surat workshop that ships finished pieces overseas, not of a US brand that also looks after the customer afterwards.

## Customers love the stone and resent the wait

Reputation is positive but thin. Trustpilot is the main hub. Snapshots range from **4.5 on 37-38 reviews** on country mirrors to **4.8 on 62** and **4.8 on 110**. Indexed page counts of up to "6 of 11" suggest roughly 200 reviews by 2026 ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com); [Trustpilot CA](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com); [page 6 of 11](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com?page=6)). Smaller aggregators show **4.0 on 28 reviews** at SmartCustomer and 5 stars on 8 ratings at RealReviews ([SmartCustomer](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/caratbee.com); [RealReviews](https://realreviews.io/reviews/caratbee.com)). Researchers found no BBB or Sitejabber listing, no independent YouTube reviews, no lawsuits or FTC actions, and no Reddit coverage (Reddit could not be searched from this environment). Automated trust scores disagree: Gridinsoft gives 95/100, ScamAdviser says "likely legit", and Scam Detector rates it "medium-low" ([Gridinsoft](https://gridinsoft.com/online-virus-scanner/url/caratbee-com); [ScamAdviser](https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website/caratbee.com); [Scam Detector](https://www.scam-detector.com/validator/caratbee-com-review/)). Look-alike domains such as caratbee.co.uk score as "not likely legitimate", which risks confusing buyers ([Scam Detector .co.uk](https://www.scam-detector.com/validator/caratbee-co-uk-review/)).

The praise is consistent. Rings "look even better in person than in provided photos", feel sturdy and sparkle strongly, and reviewers rate the value highly. Staff "keep in touch every step of the way" by phone, text and email, and help with custom designs ([SmartCustomer](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/caratbee.com); [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com)). The complaints centre on **lead-time reliability**. One buyer paid for expedited shipping for a proposal and reported the ring "sat in 'Crafting' for 10 days with zero communication". They described being "repeatedly fed lies and given empty promises about completion dates" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com)). Another reviewer alleged that Caratbee **offered free earrings on condition that they modify or remove a negative review**. After Caratbee rushed the order, refunded the express fee and paid the import duty, that reviewer raised the rating from 1 to 3 stars ([Trustpilot CA p.2](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/caratbee.com?page=2)). The snippets do not show whether these were the same buyer. The allegation is unproven. If true, it would breach Trustpilot's rules and the FTC's 2024 rule on fake and incentivised reviews. Caratbee does reply publicly and recovers generously, but it recovers after problems happen instead of preventing them. For engagement rings, the proposal date is part of what the buyer is paying for, so a missed date outweighs any sparkle.

## Seven gaps a new Indian gold brand can exploit

Caratbee confirms the parts of the model that work. Surat supply, IGI paperwork, 10K/14K/18K options, a US LLC that unlocks BNPL, Shopify plus Etsy, and price-intent search (SEO plus Google Ads) can build a profitable-looking niche without spending on social. Its weaknesses line up with what a new brand planning rings, chains, pendants and earrings should do differently:

| Caratbee today | Opening for a new entrant |
|---|---|
| Per site policy, customer pays ~15.5-16% duty plus broker fees at the door (ads now say "no hidden fees"; unverified); a refused parcel gets no refund | **DDP pricing** with duty built in and "no fees at delivery" as a headline promise; later, bulk import through a US entity acting as importer of record ([FR 2026-12670](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other)) |

Some of these gaps come with trade-offs. Caratbee's low prices and made-to-order model mean it holds almost no inventory, carries no duty risk, and loses nothing on duty when goods are returned. A duty-paid entrant has to add roughly $70-90 of duty to a 1ct solitaire and absorb that cost on 12-20% of orders that come back ([Corso](https://www.corso.com/post-purchase-resource-center/ecommerce-return-rate-benchmarks-by-industry)). Stocking in the US brings back the inventory and gold-price risk that Caratbee avoids. Caratbee's search-led acquisition (Google Ads, SEO and Etsy) is also a real advantage, because it reaches buyers who are already looking, and paid social struggles to match it, since jewellery on Meta typically returns only 1.8-2.5x ad spend ([Mako Metrics](https://makometrics.com/blog/facebook-ads-roas-benchmark)). The right response is to copy the parts of Caratbee's model that save money (a Surat SEZ or contract manufacturing, Etsy plus Shopify, content SEO with Google Shopping and Search ads bidding on high-intent terms, a US LLC for payments) and spend the savings on the moments that build trust: a delivered price with no surprises, a guaranteed date, and easy returns and resizing.

## Conclusion

Caratbee's lesson is less about any one tactic and more about where its margin comes from. Much of its price advantage comes from costs it pushes onto the customer rather than removes: import duty, delivery risk, resizing and after-sale care. That worked under de minimis. It works less well now that every parcel carries a 15.5-16% duty bill and US shoppers compare total delivered cost. A new brand that absorbs those costs openly will look more expensive on the product page and cheaper at the door. That is the comparison US jewellery buyers, especially gift buyers, actually make.

The second insight is that the field is still wide open. After six years, the most visible Surat-to-US direct brand in this search has about 200 reviews and 5,000 followers, and its paid marketing is Google search and shopping for engagement rings only. No India-based brand has yet claimed the everyday lab-grown gold category in the US with real brand investment. Chains, pendants and studs sit exactly where US lab-grown growth is heading, and Caratbee barely sells them. The unresolved questions about Caratbee (its revenue, its Indian legal entity, its current return window, and whether it has quietly moved to duty-paid shipping) should be checked with a test purchase and registry lookups before anyone bets heavily on its weaknesses.
